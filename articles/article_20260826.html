Mogao Grottoes Embrace AI to Preserve Millennia-Old Heritage

📅 August 26, 2026  |  📖 约980词  |  ⏱️ 建议阅读时间：15分钟

📋 文章摘要

2026年8月，敦煌研究院宣布利用人工智能和数字技术对莫高窟千年壁画进行保护与传播。通过"数字敦煌"VR展览，全球观众可沉浸式探索1:1高清复原的洞窟；AI模型基于数万张高分辨率图像实现"像素级"病害检测，能识别比头发丝还细的早期损伤。"数字藏经洞"平台已向全球发布11090件文物数字资源，吸引33个国家和地区超过25万次访问。故宫博物院、国家博物馆等机构也在全面推进文物数字化，标志着科技与文化遗产保护深度融合的新时代。

📱 When Ancient Murals Meet Digital Magic

With a single tap on a smartphone screen, celestial maidens holding lotuses, painted 1,400 years ago, spring to life and glide toward viewers from the ancient murals. At the millennia-old Mogao Grottoes in Dunhuang, northwest China's Gansu Province, cutting-edge digital immersion is doing more than offering global audiences an unprecedented journey back in time. It is also helping ensure these fragile Silk Road masterpieces survive for generations to come.

Carved into desert cliffs from 366 AD onward, the Mogao Grottoes comprise 735 caves, including 492 sanctuaries featuring Buddhist murals and painted sculptures. Inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage site in 1987, the site is recognized as the world's largest, longest continuously constructed, and most richly endowed Buddhist art repository. For centuries, these treasures have faced threats from wind erosion, sand storms, humidity, and the inevitable aging of pigment. Traditional conservation relied on physical barriers and periodic human inspection, which could only catch problems after visible damage had already occurred.

🤖 AI at the Service of Heritage

Today, local conservators are employing artificial intelligence and advanced digital tools not only to preserve these delicate relics but also to transform how the world experiences them. Through the "Discover Dunhuang" VR exhibition, visitors can enter a high-fidelity 3D reconstruction of Cave 285, where they can view ancient murals up close, explore the space in 360 degrees, and even soar above the ceiling to appreciate the early classic grotto renowned for its Sino-Western fusion and iconic flying apsaras.

Lei Zhengguang, a cultural official at the Dunhuang Academy, said the team achieved a 1:1 high-definition restoration of the caves through digital scanning and 3D reconstruction. Combined with interactive design, the project has greatly enriched the visitor experience. "Digitalization is not simply copying what already exists," Lei explained. "It is about creating a new dimension in which the past can speak directly to the present, without the barriers of distance, language, or time."

🔬 Pixel-Level Disease Detection

Behind the scenes, digitalization is quietly revolutionizing heritage conservation. In Cave 44, where complex wall curvatures pose significant challenges, teams now use automated rail systems to capture images. Ding Xiaosheng, a senior researcher in digital preservation at the Dunhuang Academy, noted that specialized software for error-free stitching of billion-pixel images and multi-source 3D reconstruction has greatly improved processing efficiency and quality.

More notably, AI models trained on tens of thousands of high-resolution images are achieving "pixel-level" disease detection. Wang Wanfu, an official at the Academy, said AI can identify early-stage damage thinner than a human hair. Through spectral analysis, the system can even give early warnings for tiny hollows in the plaster base that are completely invisible to the naked eye. "What once required months of patient, manual examination can now be accomplished in hours," Wang added. "More importantly, we can intervene before irreversible damage takes place."

🌐 Sharing Dunhuang with the World

The digital renaissance is now bringing Dunhuang to the world through the "Digital Library Cave" platform, launched globally by the Dunhuang Academy in 2025. To date, the platform has released digital resources of 11,090 manuscripts and artifacts, along with 72,810 images, attracting more than 250,000 visits from 33 countries and regions. A student in São Paulo, a scholar in Berlin, or a retiree in Tokyo can now examine the same ancient document with the same level of detail as a researcher standing in Dunhuang itself.

Dunhuang's pioneering efforts reflect a broader national push in China to safeguard cultural heritage through technology. China has updated its heritage law to encourage digital collection and utilization, while top-level guidelines and comprehensive technical standards further steer digital preservation efforts. Across the country, institutions are racing to leverage digitalization to strengthen heritage conservation. The Palace Museum has assigned "digital IDs" to over one million artifacts, offering free public access to 150,000 items via its digital relic library. Meanwhile, the National Museum of China has completed 2D imaging for 720,000 relics and 3D modeling for over 7,700, achieving replication-grade precision.

💭 Forever Young: Heritage in the Digital Age

"Next, we will deepen the integration of Dunhuang studies and digital intelligence, build a digital-intelligence research platform for Dunhuangology, and ensure that cultural relics 'remain forever young,'" said Yu Tianxiu, vice president of the Dunhuang Academy. The phrase "forever young" captures a profound aspiration: that the dialogue between past and future need not end. Technology, once seen as a threat to tradition, has become its most faithful guardian.

Yet experts caution that digital tools are not a complete substitute for the physical object. A 3D model can record every crack and pigment layer, but it cannot reproduce the atmosphere of a candle-lit cave or the texture of ancient plaster beneath one's fingertips. The goal, conservators insist, is balance — using technology to protect the original while ensuring that knowledge, beauty, and inspiration travel freely to every corner of the globe. In this balance lies the true promise of the digital renaissance: not replacing heritage, but renewing humanity's relationship with it.

🔍 长难句解析

① With a single tap on a smartphone screen, celestial maidens holding lotuses, painted 1,400 years ago, spring to life and glide toward viewers from the ancient murals.
结构分析：此句为简单句，但修饰成分丰富。句首"With a single tap on a smartphone screen"是介词短语作方式状语（意为"只需在智能手机屏幕上轻触一下"）；主语是"celestial maidens holding lotuses"，其中"holding lotuses"是现在分词短语作后置定语（修饰maidens，意为"手持莲花的飞天仙女"），其后"painted 1,400 years ago"是过去分词短语作后置定语（修饰celestial maidens，说明绘制年代）；谓语由两个并列动词构成："spring to life"和"glide toward viewers from the ancient murals"，由and连接。该句通过分词定语和并列谓语，生动描绘了数字技术让壁画"活起来"的震撼场景。
翻译：只需在智能手机屏幕上轻轻一触，1400年前绘制的、手持莲花的飞天仙女便从古老的壁画中跃然而出，向观众滑翔而来。
② Inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage site in 1987, the site is recognized as the world's largest, longest continuously constructed, and most richly endowed Buddhist art repository.
结构分析：此句为简单句。句首"Inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage site in 1987"是过去分词短语作状语（表被动，主语the site与inscribed是逻辑上的动宾关系，意为"于1987年被列入联合国教科文组织世界遗产名录"）；主句主语是"the site"，谓语是"is recognized as"（被动语态），表语是"the world's largest, longest continuously constructed, and most richly endowed Buddhist art repository"，其中三个最高级形容词largest、longest continuously constructed和most richly endowed由and并列，共同修饰Buddhist art repository。该句通过分词状语和并列最高级，简洁有力地概括了莫高窟的世界地位。
翻译：该遗址于1987年被列入联合国教科文组织世界遗产名录，被认为是世界上最大、持续建造时间最长、内涵最丰富的佛教艺术宝库。
③ Through the "Discover Dunhuang" VR exhibition, visitors can enter a high-fidelity 3D reconstruction of Cave 285, where they can view ancient murals up close, explore the space in 360 degrees, and even soar above the ceiling to appreciate the early classic grotto renowned for its Sino-Western fusion and iconic flying apsaras.
结构分析：此句为主从复合句。"Through the 'Discover Dunhuang' VR exhibition"是介词短语作方式状语；主句是"visitors can enter a high-fidelity 3D reconstruction of Cave 285"，其中"high-fidelity"是复合形容词修饰3D reconstruction；其后"where they can view ... explore ... and even soar ..."是where引导的非限制性定语从句，修饰Cave 285，从句中有三个并列动词短语（view、explore、soar），由and连接；"to appreciate the early classic grotto"是不定式短语作目的状语；"renowned for its Sino-Western fusion and iconic flying apsaras"是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰grotto。该句通过定语从句嵌套并列谓语和分词定语，完整呈现了VR展览的沉浸式体验。
翻译：通过"发现敦煌"VR展览，参观者可以进入第285窟的高保真3D重建空间，在这里他们可以近距离观看古代壁画，360度探索空间，甚至飞越窟顶上方，欣赏以中西融合和标志飞天闻名的早期经典洞窟。
④ Through spectral analysis, the system can even give early warnings for tiny hollows in the plaster base that are completely invisible to the naked eye.
结构分析：此句为主从复合句。"Through spectral analysis"是介词短语作方式状语；主句是"the system can even give early warnings for tiny hollows in the plaster base"，其中"in the plaster base"是介词短语作后置定语，修饰tiny hollows（意为"灰泥层中的微小空洞"）；其后"that are completely invisible to the naked eye"是that引导的限制性定语从句，修饰tiny hollows，其中"invisible to the naked eye"是固定搭配（意为"肉眼不可见的"）。该句结构清晰，通过方式状语和定语从句，说明了AI系统超越人眼检测能力的精确性。
翻译：通过光谱分析，该系统甚至能对灰泥层中肉眼完全不可见的微小空洞发出预警。
