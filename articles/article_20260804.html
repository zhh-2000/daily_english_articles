Quantum Computing Achieves Major Milestone: IBM Demonstrates "Quantum Advantage"

August 4, 2026 | Technology | Source: IBM Newsroom & China Daily
科技前沿 | 量子计算

文章摘要

IBM联合多家机构在量子计算领域取得重大突破，首次实现"量子优势"并建立可信验证框架。研究团队在量子硬件上成功模拟复杂量子材料，其性能超越传统超级计算机。与此同时，中国也推出了新一代光子量子计算机，标志着量子计算正从理论走向实用化。这一突破将为药物研发、材料科学等领域带来革命性变化。

A Historic Breakthrough

On July 30, 2026, IBM and its research partners announced a groundbreaking achievement in the field of quantum computing: the successful demonstration of "quantum advantage" through three independent experiments. This milestone represents a pivotal moment in the development of quantum technology, proving that quantum computers can now outperform the world's most powerful classical supercomputers in specific computational tasks.

The experiments, conducted by IBM in collaboration with Algorithmiq, Qedma, and the University of Chicago, demonstrated that quantum systems could solve complex problems more efficiently, more cheaply, or more accurately than classical methods. What makes this achievement particularly significant is that the research team has established a framework for trusting quantum computation results even when classical verification is impossible.

Understanding Quantum Advantage

Quantum advantage occurs when a quantum computer can perform a task that classical computers cannot complete in a reasonable timeframe. In these experiments, researchers simulated heterogeneous quantum materials on IBM's Quantum Heron processor. The results remained unmatched by classical simulation methods for eight months, demonstrating the practical superiority of quantum computing.

The team developed a model of heterogeneous quantum matter in which information propagates through regions with different local properties. This type of simulation is crucial for understanding real-world materials, including catalysts and battery electrolytes. By executing these simulations on quantum hardware, researchers gained insights that would be virtually impossible to obtain through traditional computing methods.

Building Trust in Quantum Results

One of the central challenges in quantum computing has been verifying results when no classical computer can check them. The research team addressed this issue by developing an innovative approach involving noise manipulation and comprehensive error mitigation techniques. By deliberately modifying the noise affecting quantum circuits and testing across multiple IBM Quantum processors, they demonstrated that their results remained stable and trustworthy.

This verification framework represents a blueprint for future scientific discovery using quantum computers. It provides researchers with a methodology to validate quantum results without relying solely on classical comparison, opening new possibilities for exploring complex physical phenomena.

China's Quantum Progress

While IBM made headlines with its quantum advantage demonstration, China has also achieved significant progress in quantum computing. In June 2026, China Telecom Quantum Group launched the Tianyan-P2000 photonic quantum computer, which began formal operation and connected to China's Tianyan quantum cloud platform. This system can control 2,682 photons and completed a high-complexity computation in just 29 microseconds—a task that would take classical supercomputers approximately 16 billion years.

Photonic quantum computing offers several advantages over superconducting systems, including room-temperature operation, longer coherence times, lower noise levels, and direct integration with existing fiber-optic networks. The launch of Tianyan-P2000 positions China as a leader in quantum computing technology, providing online quantum advantage services to researchers worldwide.

Implications for the Future

These breakthroughs signal that quantum computing is transitioning from theoretical possibility to practical reality. The technology promises to revolutionize numerous fields, including drug discovery, materials science, financial modeling, and artificial intelligence. As quantum systems continue to scale and improve, they may eventually solve problems that are fundamentally impossible for classical computers to address.

The race for quantum supremacy has intensified, with major technology companies and nations investing heavily in quantum research. For high school students interested in science and technology, understanding quantum computing fundamentals will become increasingly important as this technology reshapes our digital landscape in the coming decades.

长难句解析

1. On July 30, 2026, IBM and its research partners announced a groundbreaking achievement in the field of quantum computing: the successful demonstration of "quantum advantage" through three independent experiments.
句子结构分析：

主句：IBM and its research partners announced a groundbreaking achievement（IBM及其研究合作伙伴宣布了一项突破性成就）。介词短语作状语：On July 30, 2026（时间状语）、in the field of quantum computing（地点状语）。冒号后为同位语，对achievement进行解释说明。

译文：2026年7月30日，IBM及其研究合作伙伴宣布了量子计算领域的一项突破性成就：通过三项独立实验成功展示了"量子优势"。
2. What makes this achievement particularly significant is that the research team has established a framework for trusting quantum computation results even when classical verification is impossible.
句子结构分析：

主句：What makes this achievement particularly significant is that...（让这一成就特别重要的是……）。What引导主语从句作主语，that引导表语从句。表语从句中：the research team has established a framework（研究团队建立了一个框架）为核心结构，for trusting quantum computation results为目的状语，even when classical verification is impossible为让步状语从句。

译文：让这一成就特别重要的是，研究团队建立了一个框架，即使在进行传统验证不可能的情况下，也能信任量子计算的结果。
3. By executing these simulations on quantum hardware, researchers gained insights that would be virtually impossible to obtain through traditional computing methods.
句子结构分析：

主句：researchers gained insights（研究人员获得了见解）。By executing these simulations on quantum hardware为方式状语（通过在量子硬件上执行这些模拟）。that would be virtually impossible to obtain through traditional computing methods为定语从句，修饰insights，其中virtual impossible为形容词短语，to obtain...为不定式短语作状语。

译文：通过在量子硬件上执行这些模拟，研究人员获得了通过传统计算方法几乎不可能获得的见解。
4. This system can control 2,682 photons and completed a high-complexity computation in just 29 microseconds—a task that would take classical supercomputers approximately 16 billion years.
句子结构分析：

主句：This system can control... and completed...（该系统能够控制……并完成了……），为并列谓语结构。破折号后为同位语，对前面的computation进行解释说明。that引导定语从句修饰task，从句中would take classical supercomputers approximately 16 billion years为谓语部分。

译文：该系统能够控制2,682个光子，并在仅29微秒内完成了一项高复杂度计算——这项任务如果让传统超级计算机来完成，大约需要160亿年。
