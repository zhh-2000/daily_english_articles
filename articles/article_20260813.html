Record-Breaking Heat: 2026 Marks Hottest Summer on Record

📅 2026年8月13日 📚 来源：World Meteorological Organization
时政类 · 气候变化

📋 文章摘要

2026年夏季成为全球有气象记录以来最热的夏天，7月份全球平均气温比工业化前水平高出1.47°C，与2024年7月并列为史上最热的月份。西欧经历了前所未有的连续五次热浪，英国气温达到38°C，加拿大野火释放的烟雾影响了超过1.2亿人口。世界气象组织警告称，极端高温、干旱和野火已成为北半球夏季的标志性特征，气候变化正在加速这些极端天气事件的频率和强度。

Record-Breaking Global Temperatures

The summer of 2026 has officially become the hottest on record globally, according to the World Meteorological Organization (WMO). July 2026 tied with July 2024 as the warmest month ever recorded since 1850, with global average surface air temperatures reaching 1.47°C above the pre-industrial baseline (1850-1900 average). This milestone marks a significant escalation in the ongoing climate crisis that scientists have been warning about for decades.

What makes this year particularly alarming is the persistence and intensity of extreme weather events across multiple continents. North America, Africa, and Asia all experienced their warmest July on record, while Western Europe faced an unprecedented series of five consecutive heatwaves between May and August. The UK's Met Office recorded temperatures climbing to 38°C (100°F) across central and southeastern England, narrowly missing the August 2003 record of 38.5°C.

Infrastructure Struggles Under Heat Stress

The exceptional heat has severely strained infrastructure and public services across Europe, particularly in countries that were historically designed for cooler climates. In Britain, hospital staff experienced heat-related collapses and illnesses severe enough to require admission, drawing sharp criticism from healthcare unions. The Royal College of Nursing warned that healthcare workers are "being failed" by workplace conditions unable to manage extreme temperatures.

Transportation systems have also been significantly impacted. Major rivers across Europe, including the Seine, Rhine, and Danube, reached exceptionally low levels, affecting water supply, irrigation, and energy production in multiple countries. The persistent drought has created water shortages across much of Western Europe, affecting households, farms, and natural ecosystems. Southern Water in England requested a rare drought order that would restrict non-essential water uses, marking the first such restriction since 2006.

Wildfires and Air Quality Crisis

Canada's 2026 wildfire season has proven exceptionally severe, with more than 3 million hectares burned to date. What was particularly remarkable this year was the exceptionally rapid rate at which burned area accumulated over a very short period of time—approximately 94% of the total area burned occurred during just five weeks between the end of June and end of July.

The consequences extended far beyond Canada's borders. Over 120 million people in the Midwest and Northeast United States were affected by dangerous air quality, with alerts stretching from Minnesota to New Hampshire. Exposure to elevated concentrations of wildfire smoke has been associated with increased emergency healthcare utilization, particularly for respiratory and cardiovascular conditions, as well as increases in all-cause mortality.

Scientific Attribution to Climate Change

A comprehensive study by scientists from Canada, the US, the Netherlands, and the UK has determined that human-induced climate change has significantly altered the likelihood and intensity of the weather conditions driving these fires. In Ontario, the rapid spread and establishment of fires was made approximately 3.5 times more likely by 1.4°C of global warming. The research team found that events that would have occurred once every 50-100 years in a cooler climate now happen every 2-6 years.

"What we have seen over the last few months is the combined effect of long-term warming with a persistent pattern of alternating high- and low-pressure systems stretching across the northern hemisphere," explains John Kennedy, head of climate information at WMO. "These kinds of high-pressure patterns can last days or even, as we have seen this year, for weeks."

Call for Urgent Action

The continuing series of high-impact weather and climate events underscores the urgent need for reliable forecasts, early warning systems, and coordinated international response. The WMO emphasizes that limiting the cascading impacts on society and economies requires sustained investment in climate monitoring, adaptation measures, and decisive action to reduce greenhouse gas emissions.

As temperatures continue to rise, experts warn that infrastructure designed for historical climate conditions will increasingly fail to protect populations from extreme heat events. The summer of 2026 serves as a stark reminder that climate change is not a distant threat but a present reality requiring immediate and sustained global action.

🔍 长难句分析（4个）

July 2026 tied with July 2024 as the warmest month ever recorded since 1850, with global average surface air temperatures reaching 1.47°C above the pre-industrial baseline (1850-1900 average).
句子结构分析：
• 主句：July 2026 tied with July 2024 as the warmest month
• 过去分词短语作定语：ever recorded since 1850（修饰 month）
• with复合结构：with global average surface air temperatures reaching...（表伴随状态）
• 介词短语：above the pre-industrial baseline（表示比较）
翻译：2026年7月与2024年7月并列为自1850年有记录以来最热的月份，全球平均地表气温比工业化前基准线（1850-1900年平均值）高出1.47°C。
The exceptional heat has severely strained infrastructure and public services across Europe, particularly in countries that were historically designed for cooler climates.
句子结构分析：
• 主句：The exceptional heat has severely strained infrastructure and public services
• 介词短语：across Europe（表地点范围）
• 定语从句：that were historically designed for cooler climates（修饰 countries）
翻译：极端高温严重冲击了欧洲各地的基础设施和公共服务，特别是在那些历史上为凉爽气候设计的国家。
Exposure to elevated concentrations of wildfire smoke has been associated with increased emergency healthcare utilization, particularly for respiratory and cardiovascular conditions, as well as increases in all-cause mortality.
句子结构分析：
• 主语：Exposure to elevated concentrations of wildfire smoke
• 谓语：has been associated with（现在完成时被动语态）
• 宾语：increased emergency healthcare utilization 和 increases in all-cause mortality
• 插入语：particularly for respiratory and cardiovascular conditions

The continuing series of high-impact weather and climate events underscores the urgent need for reliable forecasts, early warning systems, and coordinated international response to limit the cascading impacts on society and economies.

• 主句：The continuing series...underscores the urgent need
