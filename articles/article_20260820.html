社会热点 | 乡村振兴

China's Rural Revitalization: New Momentum in a Changing Economy

📅 August 20, 2026  |  📖 约980词  |  ⏱️ 建议阅读时间：15分钟

📋 文章摘要

2026年，中国乡村振兴战略持续推进，乡村经济焕发全新活力。一季度全国乡村旅游接待7.93亿人次，收入4570亿元，同比增长12.2%。从长江江心岛的咖啡馆到徽州古村的研学旅游，从乡村电商直播到跨境入境游，农村正从单一农业生产转向文旅融合的多元发展，为年轻人创造了在家门口就业的新机遇。

🌾 A Quiet Island Transformed

Six years ago, a small island in the Yangtze River in east China was very quiet and rarely visited by young people. Visitors arriving by ferry were greeted by little more than an empty pier and winding roads. Today, the island in Xinzhou Township of Anhui Province tells a completely different story. Upgraded roads cross the landscape, colorful murals brighten village homes, and cafes, boutique homestays and rural restaurants have popped up everywhere.

With just over 1,000 people living there year-round, the island drew more than 8,000 visitors during this year's May Day holiday, many of them young people. Among the island's top attractions is a coffee shop converted from a two-story village house. For Zhao Lei, who grew up in the city and now runs the cafe, it all began with her first visit to the island in May last year. "The river breeze and the endless fields really struck me," she said. To her, they offered a distinctive beauty of the countryside and a slower pace of life that city life could not easily replicate.

📊 Impressive Numbers Behind the Trend

The story of the once-forgotten island offers a glimpse into the broader changes taking place across China's vast countryside, where more than 450 million people reside. Launched in 2017, the rural revitalization strategy has paved the way for policies that support unique rural industries, greater integration of agriculture with other sectors, and exciting new business models.

The results are remarkable. In the first quarter of 2026, rural tourism destinations across China received an estimated 793 million visits, up 12.2 percent year on year, generating 457 billion yuan (about 67.3 billion U.S. dollars) in revenue, up 10.9 percent, according to the Ministry of Culture and Tourism. To date, 8,155 villages have been included on the national list of protected traditional villages, while more than 12,900 intangible cultural heritage workshops have been established, directly creating jobs for more than one million people.

🏛️ Heritage as an Engine of Development

Increasingly, villages are tapping into their local cultural heritage to create new opportunities for development. This summer, Xidi Village, a UNESCO World Heritage Site in Huangshan City, has seen study tours become a major draw, offering children a hands-on way to explore its distinctive architecture and traditional culture. Known for its well-preserved residential buildings from the Ming and Qing dynasties, Xidi features ancient homes adorned with meaningful couplets that reflect centuries-old values of learning, morality and harmony.

A recently aired TV drama centered on Huizhou ink-making, a craft with a history spanning more than 1,000 years, has further drawn visitors to its filming locations in Xidi. Visitors can pick up drama-themed souvenirs, dress in styles inspired by their favorite characters, and even sample dishes featured in the drama. As both the birthplace and a major hub of Huizhou culture, Huangshan has seamlessly woven its rich intangible heritage into village tourism. Visitors can try traditional crafts like woodcarving and seal engraving, or join locals in a lively bench dragon dance after nightfall.

📱 Digital Empowerment in the Countryside

Digital technology is also reshaping the rural landscape. In Gansu Province, farmers on the Loess Plateau have replaced plowshares with smartphone gimbals. In rural courtyards, local women promote handmade vinegar and snacks to thousands of viewers in their native dialects, while young entrepreneurs broadcast the plateau's seasonal cycle to the world. The hamlet of Zhuangzimao, home to only 22 households, saw its total sales of local specialties exceed three million yuan last year, with over half of the families earning an annual income of more than 100,000 yuan.

In Zhejiang Province, the village collective of Tahou developed its own WeChat mini-program, integrating over 70 homestays under a unified booking and service-quality score system. Supported by local government initiatives and platforms, village-run studios train locals to sell locally produced mugwort pillows and wellness tourism packages directly to urban consumers. In these cases, digital tools did not replace the village; they granted villagers ownership of the customer relationship, turning passive scenery into profitable business.

🌍 Rural Tourism Meets the World

The rural turn is not limited to domestic tourists. Overseas visitors who once contented themselves with city walks in Beijing and Shanghai are now boarding early-morning buses bound for rural areas, walking into tea terraces, ancient pear orchards and Miao embroidery workshops. Behind this trend are practical enablers: relaxed visa waivers, smoother payment and language services, and a rural transport grid that now reaches once-isolated valleys.

China now has 19 villages on the UN Tourism "Best Tourism Villages" list, the most for any country, after the 2025 round added Digang in Zhejiang, Huanggang in Guizhou, Jikayi in Sichuan and Dongluo in Jiangsu. In Shichuan town, Gansu Province, the ancient pear orchard system, home to more than 9,400 pear trees over a century old, was inscribed as a Globally Important Agricultural Heritage Systems site. During the 2026 pear-blossom season, the town drew over one million visitors, and more than 85 percent of local households now earn from tourism-related work.

🔮 Looking Ahead: Multiple Functions of Agriculture

"Developing the multiple functions of agriculture" has been incorporated as a measure in the outline of China's 15th Five-Year Plan (2026-2030), referring to efforts to ensure food security and supply while integrating agriculture with culture and tourism, and expanding the functions of rural areas in ecological conservation, leisure and recreation, and cultural heritage. Experts believe the measure will encourage local governments over the next five years to tap the multiple functions of agriculture and unlock the diverse value of rural areas, creating stronger foundations for farmers to increase their incomes and for young people to build careers close to home.

🔍 长难句解析

① Launched in 2017, the rural revitalization strategy has paved the way for policies that support unique rural industries, greater integration of agriculture with other sectors and exciting new business models.
结构分析：句首"Launched in 2017"是过去分词短语作状语，表示被动含义（战略于2017年启动）；主句主语是"the rural revitalization strategy"，谓语是"has paved"（现在完成时），宾语是"the way"；"for policies"是介词短语修饰way；其后"that support..."是that引导的定语从句修饰policies，从句谓语是support，后接三个并列宾语：unique rural industries、greater integration of agriculture with other sectors和exciting new business models。
翻译： launched于2017年的乡村振兴战略，为支持独特乡村产业、农业与其他产业更深层次融合以及令人振奋的新商业模式等政策铺平了道路。
② In the first quarter of 2026, rural tourism destinations across China received an estimated 793 million visits, up 12.2 percent year on year, generating 457 billion yuan in revenue, up 10.9 percent, according to the Ministry of Culture and Tourism.
结构分析：句首"In the first quarter of 2026"是时间状语；主句主语是"rural tourism destinations"，谓语是"received"，宾语是"an estimated 793 million visits"；"up 12.2 percent year on year"是形容词短语作伴随状语，表示同比增长；"generating 457 billion yuan in revenue"是现在分词短语作结果状语；"up 10.9 percent"同样是形容词短语补充说明revenue的增长；句末"according to the Ministry of Culture and Tourism"是介词短语作信息来源状语。
翻译：据文化和旅游部数据，2026年第一季度，全国乡村旅游目的地接待估计7.93亿人次，同比增长12.2%，创造收入4570亿元，增长10.9%。
③ In these cases, digital tools did not replace the village; they granted villagers ownership of the customer relationship, turning passive scenery into interactive, profitable business.
结构分析：此句由分号连接两个并列分句。第一个分句主语是"digital tools"，谓语是"did not replace"，宾语是"the village"。第二个分句主语是"they"（指代digital tools），谓语是"granted"，宾语是"villagers"（双宾语的间接宾语），"ownership of the customer relationship"是直接宾语；句末"turning passive scenery into interactive, profitable business"是现在分词短语作结果状语，其中turn...into...为固定搭配，interactive和profitable是并列形容词修饰business。
翻译：在这些案例中，数字工具并没有取代乡村；它们让村民拥有了客户关系的掌控权，将被动的风景转化为互动的、有利可图的商业。
④ Experts believe the measure will encourage local governments over the next five years to tap the multiple functions of agriculture and unlock the diverse value of rural areas, creating stronger foundations for farmers to increase their incomes.
结构分析：主句是"Experts believe (that)..."，that引导宾语从句（此处省略了that）。宾语从句主语是"the measure"，谓语是"will encourage"，宾语是"local governments"；"over the next five years"是时间状语；其后"to tap...and unlock..."是两个并列的不定式短语作宾语补足语（encourage sb to do sth结构）；"creating stronger foundations"是现在分词短语作结果状语；"for farmers to increase their incomes"是介词短语，其中不定式"to increase their incomes"作farmers的宾语补足语。
翻译：专家认为，这项措施将在未来五年鼓励地方政府开发农业的多种功能，释放乡村的多元价值，为农民增收创造更坚实的基础。
