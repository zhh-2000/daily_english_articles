Iron-Air Batteries: A Breakthrough in Renewable Energy Storage

📅 August 18, 2026  |  📖 约900词  |  ⏱️ 建议阅读时间：15分钟

📋 文章摘要

荷兰初创公司Ore Energy成功研发铁空气电池技术，获4300万美元融资。该电池利用铁的生锈与除锈反应储存能量，可供电100小时，成本仅为锂电池的十分之一，不依赖锂、钴等稀有金属。这项突破解决了可再生能源存储瓶颈，为AI数据中心和工业发展提供稳定、清洁的基荷电力。

🔋 The Storage Challenge

As the world transitions toward renewable energy, one critical challenge remains unsolved: how to store wind and solar power efficiently for extended periods. Wind turbines and solar panels generate electricity only when conditions are favorable, but energy demand continues around the clock. This mismatch between generation and consumption has become one of the biggest obstacles in the global energy transition.

According to the European Commission's Joint Research Centre, Europe wastes approximately 72 terawatt-hours (TWh) of renewable electricity annually due to grid bottlenecks—enough to power Austria for an entire year. By 2040, these losses are projected to reach 410 TWh annually. The United Kingdom alone has spent nearly £6 billion since 2011 paying renewable generators to switch off when the grid cannot accommodate their output.

⚡ Breakthrough Technology

Ore Energy, an Amsterdam-based startup, has developed a revolutionary solution: iron-air batteries that can store electricity for up to 100 hours at significantly lower costs than conventional lithium-ion systems. The technology operates on a simple yet ingenious principle—rusting and unrusting iron electrodes using only iron, water, and air.

During the discharge phase, the battery inhales oxygen from the air, causing the iron to rust. When charging, the process reverses: electrical current converts the rust back into pure iron while releasing oxygen. This reversible rusting cycle can repeat thousands of times without degradation, making it ideal for grid-scale energy storage.

🏭 Manufacturing Advantages

Unlike lithium-ion batteries that require critical minerals like lithium, cobalt, and nickel—often sourced from geopolitically sensitive regions—iron-air batteries use materials that are abundant and inexpensive. Iron is one of the most common elements on Earth, costing a fraction of battery-grade lithium. This material advantage translates to energy storage costs approximately ten times lower per unit of capacity compared to lithium-ion systems for long-duration applications.

Furthermore, Ore Energy's manufacturing process relies entirely on European supply chains, eliminating dependence on imported critical raw materials. This independence addresses growing concerns about supply chain security and sustainability in the battery industry.

💡 Meeting Rising Demand

The timing of this innovation couldn't be more crucial. Global electricity demand from data centers is projected to more than double to approximately 945 TWh by 2030, with AI-optimized facilities expected to quadruple. AI training and inference operations create rapid, substantial swings in power demand, making reliable storage essential for maintaining stable electricity supplies.

Ore Energy has already secured a landmark agreement with Budget Thuis, a Dutch utility company, to deploy 1 gigawatt-hour (GWh) of iron-air storage capacity. The company has also completed successful pilot projects with French utility EDF, demonstrating real-world performance in utility settings. With Series A funding totaling $43 million from investors including Plural and HV, Ore Energy aims to establish its first manufacturing facility and achieve gigawatt-hour-scale production by 2028.

🌍 Environmental Impact

Beyond economic benefits, iron-air technology offers substantial environmental advantages. The batteries contain no toxic materials and present minimal fire risk compared to lithium-ion alternatives. At the end of their operational life, the iron components can be fully recycled, supporting circular economy principles. By enabling longer storage durations, this technology helps maximize the utilization of existing renewable infrastructure, reducing the need for fossil fuel backup power plants.

Industry experts believe iron-air batteries could become the standard infrastructure for long-duration energy storage by 2035, fundamentally transforming how societies power their economies and positioning renewable energy as a reliable, round-the-clock power source.

🔍 长难句解析

① Unlike lithium-ion batteries that require critical minerals like lithium, cobalt, and nickel—often sourced from geopolitically sensitive regions—iron-air batteries use materials that are abundant and inexpensive.
结构分析：主句主语是"iron-air batteries"，谓语是"use"。句首"Unlike lithium-ion batteries"作比较状语，后接that引导的定语从句修饰batteries；破折号内是插入语，补充说明minerals的来源；主句宾语materials后接that引导的定语从句。
翻译：与需要锂、钴、镍等关键矿物的锂电池不同——这些矿物往往来自地缘政治敏感地区——铁空气电池使用的是丰富且廉价的材料。
② AI training and inference operations create rapid, substantial swings in power demand, making reliable storage essential for maintaining stable electricity supplies.
结构分析：主句主语是"AI training and inference operations"，谓语是"create"，宾语是"swings"。后半部分是现在分词短语作结果状语，其中"essential"是宾语补足语，说明storage的状态。
翻译：AI训练和推理操作会造成电力需求的快速、大幅波动，使得可靠的储能对维持稳定的电力供应至关重要。
③ By enabling longer storage durations, this technology helps maximize the utilization of existing renewable infrastructure, reducing the need for fossil fuel backup power plants.
结构分析："By enabling..."是介词短语作方式状语；主句主语是"this technology"，谓语是"helps maximize"（help后接不带to的不定式）；"reducing..."是现在分词短语作结果状语，表示技术带来的附加效果。
翻译：通过实现更长的储能时间，这项技术有助于最大化现有可再生能源基础设施的利用率，从而减少对化石燃料备用发电厂的需求。
④ Industry experts believe iron-air batteries could become the standard infrastructure for long-duration energy storage by 2035, fundamentally transforming how societies power their economies.
结构分析：主句是"Industry experts believe..."，后接省略that的宾语从句；宾语从句中主语是"iron-air batteries"，谓语是"could become"；"fundamentally transforming..."是现在分词短语作结果状语，其中how引导宾语从句作transforming的宾语。
翻译：行业专家认为，到2035年铁空气电池可能成为长时储能的标准基础设施，从根本上改变社会为经济供能的方式。
