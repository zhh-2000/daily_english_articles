Brain Possesses Greater Self-Repair Capacity Than Previously Assumed

大脑的自我修复能力远超预期

neuroscience biology research

Source: University of Zurich | August 10, 2026

📋 文章摘要

瑞士苏黎世大学研究团队发现，成年大脑具有比预期更强的自我修复能力。研究人员在小鼠大脑中识别出一类特殊的"再生性星形胶质细胞"，它们能够通过细胞核迁移的方式修复受损脑组织。这一发现发表在《自然·神经科学》期刊上，为脑损伤和神经系统疾病的治疗开辟了新方向。

The Discovery

For decades, scientists believed that the adult brain had limited capacity to repair itself after injury. This assumption was based on the observation that neurons, the primary cells responsible for transmitting information in the brain, do not readily divide after maturation. However, a groundbreaking study from the University of Zurich has fundamentally challenged this long-held belief.

Researchers at the Institute of Pharmacology and Toxicology discovered a specialized group of cells called "regenerative astrocytes" that can step in when brain tissue is damaged. Astrocytes are star-shaped glial cells that provide crucial support to neurons, supplying them with nutrients, regulating blood flow, and maintaining the health of brain tissue. Previously, it was thought that when these supporting cells were lost due to injury or autoimmune diseases, the adult brain could not fully replace them.

A Remarkable Cellular Journey

What makes this discovery particularly fascinating is the unique mechanism by which these regenerative astrocytes repair damaged tissue. Using advanced two-photon microscopy, the research team observed the brains of living mice in real time over several weeks. They discovered that these special astrocytes don't simply divide to create new cells at the injury site. Instead, they perform a remarkable feat: they send newly formed cell nuclei gliding across long distances through their star-shaped cellular extensions.

The nuclei travel along these extensions, sometimes crossing considerable distances, to repopulate the damaged area of the brain and reconnect the astrocyte network. This process of nuclear translocation had never been observed before in the adult brain and represents an entirely new understanding of how neural tissue can regenerate.

Implications for Treatment

The discovery has significant implications for treating neurological conditions. Dr. Bruno Weber, who led the research team, emphasized that identifying the genes and signaling pathways activated during this repair process could serve as starting points for developing new therapeutic approaches. If these mechanisms can be selectively activated, it might help repair damaged brain tissue more effectively and improve recovery after certain brain disorders.

This finding is particularly relevant for conditions like neuromyelitis optica spectrum disorder, a rare autoimmune disease where the body's own antibodies destroy astrocytes. Understanding how the brain naturally attempts to replace these lost cells opens new possibilities for supporting recovery from such ailments.

A New Chapter in Neuroscience

This research, published in Nature Neuroscience in July 2026, represents a paradigm shift in our understanding of brain plasticity and regeneration. It demonstrates that the adult brain possesses previously unknown capabilities for self-repair, challenging the conventional view that brain injuries inevitably result in permanent damage.

The discovery also highlights the importance of continued investment in basic scientific research. By using innovative techniques like two-photon microscopy and gene mapping, researchers were able to observe cellular processes that had remained hidden from scientific view. As technology continues to advance, we can expect further revelations about the brain's remarkable capacity for adaptation and repair.

For high school students interested in neuroscience, this discovery offers an exciting glimpse into how scientific understanding evolves and how pursuing unanswered questions can lead to breakthrough findings that may ultimately benefit millions of people suffering from neurological conditions.

🔍 长难句解析

① A new study by co-lead authors Marina Herwerth and Matthias Wyss from the Institute of Pharmacology and Toxicology at the University of Zurich has now overturned that assumption: their research team headed by Bruno Weber discovered a specialized group of "regenerative" astrocytes in the brains of living mice that step in on the perimeter of the damaged area of the brain to rebuild the cells.

句型结构：复合句，包含定语从句和目的状语

主干：A new study... has overturned that assumption

修饰成分：by co-lead authors...（介词短语作后置定语修饰study）；headed by Bruno Weber（过去分词短语作后置定语修饰team）；that step in...（定语从句修饰astrocytes）

译文：苏黎世大学药理学与毒理学研究所的共同第一作者Marina Herwerth和Matthias Wyss发表的一项新研究推翻了这一假设：由Bruno Weber领导的研究团队在活体小鼠的大脑中发现了一类特殊的"再生性"星形胶质细胞，它们会在受损脑区的边缘介入，重建细胞。

② The researchers used two-photon microscopy to observe the brains of living mice in real time over a period of several weeks and mapped which genes switch on in which areas of the brain, enabling them to identify the special astrocytes that take care of rebuilding injured tissue.

句型结构：并列复合句，含宾语从句和定语从句

主干：The researchers used... to observe... and mapped which genes...

修饰成分：in real time（时间状语）；over a period of several weeks（时间状语）；which genes switch on in which areas（宾语从句）；enabling them to identify...（现在分词短语作结果状语）；that take care of...（定语从句修饰astrocytes）

译文：研究人员使用双光子显微镜在几周内实时观察活体小鼠的大脑，并绘制了哪些基因在大脑的哪些区域被激活的图谱，从而使他们能够识别出负责重建受损组织的特殊星形胶质细胞。

③ The discovery of how adult brain cell nuclei migrate through the long star-shaped extensions of astrocytes to injured tissue expands our comprehension of how the brain organizes and regenerates itself after certain injuries.

句型结构：简单句，含多个宾语从句

主干：The discovery... expands our comprehension...

修饰成分：of how adult brain cell nuclei migrate...（介词短语作后置定语修饰discovery）；through the long star-shaped extensions（介词短语表路径）；of how the brain organizes and regenerates...（介词短语作后置定语修饰comprehension）

译文：关于成人大脑细胞核如何通过星形胶质细胞的长星状突起迁移到受损组织的发现，拓展了我们对大脑在特定损伤后如何组织和再生自身的理解。

④ If those mechanisms can be selectively activated, that could help to more effectively repair damaged brain tissue, restore astrocyte networks and thus improve recovery after certain brain disorders.

句型结构：条件状语从句 + 主句（并列谓语）

主干：If... that could help to repair..., restore... and thus improve...

修饰成分：If those mechanisms can be selectively activated（条件状语从句）；more effectively（副词修饰repair）；damaged（过去分词作定语修饰tissue）；thus（连接副词表结果）

译文：如果这些机制能够被选择性激活，将有助于更有效地修复受损的脑组织，恢复星形胶质细胞网络，从而改善特定脑部疾病后的恢复情况。
