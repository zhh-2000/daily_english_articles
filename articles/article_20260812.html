Chinese Team Sets World Record in Solar Cell Efficiency

📅 August 12, 2026 | 📰 Global Times
🔬 Science & Technology

📌 文章摘要

中国科学院化学研究所团队在钙钛矿-有机叠层太阳能电池研究中取得重大突破，通过创新的"全阶段调控"策略，成功解决了长期困扰该领域的光致相分离难题。团队研发的太阳能电池经认证效率达到28.04%，创下新的世界纪录。这一突破为下一代光伏技术的商业化铺平了道路，有望将太阳能应用拓展至建筑一体化光伏、便携式能源设备乃至未来空间动力系统等领域。

📖 正文阅读

Light is the source of energy for solar cells, but it can also become a factor accelerating performance degradation. For wide-bandgap subcells in perovskite-organic tandem solar cells, which have long been troubled by light-induced phase segregation, Chinese researchers have developed a new strategy to overcome this challenge.

On July 13 Beijing time, the international journal Nature published the latest research achievement from a team led by academician Li Yongfang and research fellow Meng Lei from the Institute of Chemistry under the Chinese Academy of Sciences. The breakthrough marks a significant step forward in overcoming a bottleneck limiting the commercialization of next-generation photovoltaic technologies.

The team proposed a "full-stage regulation" strategy that suppresses harmful phase separation throughout the entire lifecycle of perovskite materials—from fabrication to operation. The perovskite-organic tandem solar cell achieved a certified steady-state photoelectric conversion efficiency of 28.04 percent, setting a new world record.

The key challenge facing wide-bandgap perovskite materials is halide phase segregation. The upper perovskite layer needs both iodine and bromine ions to maximize absorption of ultraviolet and visible light, but these ions tend to separate during crystallization. "Once phase segregation occurs, the voltage of the solar cell continuously declines like air leaking from a tire, causing persistent performance degradation," Meng Lei explained.

The research team introduced a photo-convertible additive molecule called TDB, which works as a "microscopic coordinator." During fabrication, it helps iodine and bromine ions mix uniformly. During operation, it transforms into TAB, which strengthens interactions with defect sites and suppresses ion migration.

"The key technological challenge was to make high-bromine-content wide-bandgap perovskite materials transform from 'light-fearing' to 'light-harnessing'," Meng said. The wide-bandgap perovskite single-junction cell achieved an open-circuit voltage of 1.42 volts and a fill factor of 85.13 percent. Combined with an organic solar cell, the tandem device reached a laboratory efficiency of 28.80 percent and a certified steady-state efficiency of 28.04 percent.

Besides efficiency, perovskite and organic solar cells may reshape photovoltaic manufacturing. They can be produced through solution processing technologies such as roll-to-roll printing and slot-die coating, creating possibilities for lightweight and flexible energy materials. However, commercialization still faces challenges including large-area film uniformity, flexible device packaging reliability, and production consistency.

The technology also shows potential for space applications. Li said its high specific power and flexibility could help reduce the weight and volume of spacecraft power systems. "Systematic space environment simulations and in-orbit verification will be needed to evaluate their performance in radiation resistance, vacuum stability and long-term operation," Li added.

With high efficiency, lightweight structure and flexibility, perovskite-organic tandem solar cells are expected to expand solar power applications beyond traditional panels, including building-integrated photovoltaics, portable energy devices, drones and future space power systems. This breakthrough demonstrates China's leading position in renewable energy research and its commitment to sustainable development.

🔍 长难句解析

例句 1

The breakthrough marks a significant step forward in overcoming a bottleneck limiting the commercialization of next-generation photovoltaic technologies.
结构分析：主句为"The breakthrough marks a significant step forward"，介词短语"in overcoming a bottleneck"作状语，现在分词短语"limiting the commercialization..."作后置定语修饰"bottleneck"。

参考译文：这一突破标志着在克服限制下一代光伏技术商业化的瓶颈方面迈出了重要一步。

例句 2

Once phase segregation occurs, the voltage of the solar cell continuously declines like air leaking from a tire, causing persistent performance degradation.
结构分析："Once phase segregation occurs"为时间状语从句，主句为"the voltage...continuously declines"，"like air leaking from a tire"为方式状语，现在分词短语"causing persistent performance degradation"作结果状语。

参考译文：一旦发生相分离，太阳能电池的电压就会像轮胎漏气一样持续下降，导致性能持续退化。

例句 3

With high efficiency, lightweight structure and flexibility, perovskite-organic tandem solar cells are expected to expand solar power applications beyond traditional panels, including building-integrated photovoltaics, portable energy devices, drones and future space power systems.
结构分析：介词短语"With high efficiency..."作状语，主句为"perovskite-organic tandem solar cells are expected to expand..."，"beyond traditional panels"作状语，"including..."为介词短语作补充说明，列举具体应用领域。

参考译文：凭借高效率、轻质结构和柔韧性，钙钛矿-有机叠层太阳能电池有望将太阳能应用拓展到传统面板之外，包括建筑一体化光伏、便携式能源设备、无人机和未来空间动力系统等领域。

例句 4

The key technological challenge was to make high-bromine-content wide-bandgap perovskite materials transform from 'light-fearing' to 'light-harnessing'.
结构分析：主句为"The key technological challenge was to make..."，"to make..."为不定式短语作表语，其中"transform from...to..."为宾语补足语结构。

参考译文：关键的技术挑战在于使高溴含量的宽禁带钙钛矿材料从"惧光"转变为"用光"。
