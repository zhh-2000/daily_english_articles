Duplantis Clears 6.21m in Historic Pole Vault Duel in Lausanne

📅 August 22, 2026  |  📖 约950词  |  ⏱️ 建议阅读时间：15分钟

📋 文章摘要

2026年8月20日，瑞典撑杆跳名将杜普兰蒂斯在洛桑钻石联赛城市赛中以6.21米的成绩夺冠，希腊选手卡里利斯以6.16米紧随其后，两人成为历史上首次在同一场比赛中双双越过6.16米的运动员。杜普兰蒂斯随后两次冲击6.32米世界纪录未果，但这场对决已被誉为撑杆跳历史上最伟大的比赛之一。本文回顾了这场历史性较量，并介绍了杜普兰蒂斯从路易斯安那后院到世界之巅的成长之路。

🏆 A Night of History on Lake Geneva

On a warm Thursday evening, more than 5,000 spectators gathered at Place de la Navigation on the shores of Lake Geneva in Lausanne, Switzerland, to witness what would become one of the greatest pole vault competitions in history. The occasion was the city event of the Lausanne Diamond League, held on the eve of the main Athletissima meeting at the Stade Olympique de la Pontaise. What unfolded under the open sky would be remembered for years to come.

Sweden's Mondo Duplantis, the two-time Olympic champion and world record-holder, faced his greatest challenger: Greece's Emmanouil Karalis. Both athletes were perfect through their opening four heights, clearing 5.51m, 5.81m, 5.91m, and 6.01m on their first attempts. The crowd, lining the runway and clapping along to booming pop music, watched in awe as the world's best soared two storeys high above them. It was a duel that would push both men to extraordinary heights.

🤺 The Duel Unfolds

Duplantis raised the standard first, clearing 6.16m on his first attempt and adding a centimetre to his own meeting record set at the same lakeside venue in 2024. Karalis, the world silver and Olympic bronze medallist, responded brilliantly. He failed his first attempt at 6.16m but soared over on his second try, recording the best outdoor mark of his career. The two athletes had just become the first pair ever to clear 6.16m or higher in the same competition.

The bar was raised to 6.21m, and once again Duplantis sailed over at the first attempt, extending his meeting record. Karalis had two attempts at that height before making the bold decision to pass his final attempt and raise the bar directly to 6.26m — what would have been a nine-centimetre personal best. He missed, but the crowd rose to salute his courage. Duplantis then had the bar raised to 6.32m, one centimetre above his own world record set in Uppsala in March. He made two unsuccessful attempts before calling it a day.

💬 "That's What We Live For"

"I'm tired! But I feel really good, I feel really happy," said Duplantis afterwards, laughing. "That was such an amazing competition, everybody jumped so well, so I felt like I really had to step it up. I'm glad I jumped really well." The Swede, who had been beaten for the first time in three years in Stockholm in June — ending a run of 40 straight victories — and then retired from the London Diamond League in July with a groin twinge, showed that he was back in top form.

"That's competition, that's what we live for," Duplantis added. "I love to compete, I love competing against Manolo, especially when he is on his A-game like this. I just like to jump high. That's what I feel like I was made to do." Karalis was equally enthusiastic. "It's been amazing. I always love competing here," he said. "Everyone jumped very well. It's always nice to put on a good show."

🏡 From a Louisiana Backyard to the World

Duplantis's journey to greatness began far from the grand stadiums of Europe. It started in the backyard of a family home in Lafayette, Louisiana, where his father Greg — a 5.80m pole vaulter who competed for the United States — built a pole vault pit for his children. His mother Helena, a Swedish international heptathlete, also played a crucial role. Together, they have coached Duplantis throughout his career and received the World Athletics Coaching Achievement Award in 2020.

"I always felt like I was built to pole vault," Duplantis once said. "I felt like it was meant to be — for me to be the best — and that it would work out some way or another if I just stayed the path." His eldest brother Andreas was his main inspiration. "He was my idol," Mondo recalled. "Watching him grow up and be so much better than me — I just wanted to be like him and follow in my brother's footsteps."

Duplantis started pole vaulting at the age of four and set the first in a series of world age bests when he was seven, clearing 2.33m. By the time he was 17, he had already jumped 5.90m. In February 2020, at the age of 20, he broke the world record for the first time in Torun, Poland, clearing 6.17m and ending Frenchman Renaud Lavillenie's six-year hold on the record. Since then, he has broken the world record an astonishing 15 times, with his current mark of 6.31m set in front of his home club in Uppsala in March 2026.

🤝 Camaraderie and the Spirit of Sport

One of the most remarkable aspects of the pole vault community is the camaraderie among its top competitors. Duplantis grew up with a signed poster of Lavillenie on his bedroom wall, only to succeed him as world record-holder. He credits the sport's veterans with helping him reach another level. "From the very beginning they showed me nothing but respect and just treated me like one of the other guys," he remembered of his first competition against the elite at age 17.

"I know that the only way to be the best is if you compete against the best — 'iron sharpens iron' kind of stuff," Duplantis explained. "I wanted to go out there and not just dip my toes in it, I wanted to dive headfirst and compete at the biggest meet with the biggest guys." That philosophy has defined his career, and it was on full display in Lausanne, where he and Karalis embraced warmly after the competition, celebrating not just individual achievement but the shared pursuit of excellence.

Behind the leading pair, Australia's Kurtis Marschall finished third with a clearance of 5.91m, while Norway's Sondre Guttormsen and American Chris Nilsen also cleared 5.91m, finishing fourth and fifth respectively on countback. The historic contest served as a reminder that sport, at its finest, is not merely about winning but about the human spirit's relentless drive to reach higher — one centimetre at a time.

🔍 长难句解析

① On a warm Thursday evening, more than 5,000 spectators gathered at Place de la Navigation on the shores of Lake Geneva in Lausanne, Switzerland, to witness what would become one of the greatest pole vault competitions in history.
结构分析：句首"On a warm Thursday evening"是介词短语作时间状语；主句主语是"more than 5,000 spectators"，谓语是"gathered"；其后"at Place de la Navigation"是地点状语，"on the shores of Lake Geneva in Lausanne, Switzerland"是更具体的位置定语修饰Place；"to witness what would become one of the greatest pole vault competitions in history"是不定式短语作目的状语，其中"what would become..."是what引导的宾语从句作witness的宾语，从句中"one of the greatest pole vault competitions in history"是become的表语。
翻译：在一个温暖的周四傍晚，超过5000名观众聚集在瑞士洛桑日内瓦湖畔的航海广场，见证了一场将成为历史上最伟大的撑杆跳比赛之一的赛事。
② Duplantis, who had been beaten for the first time in three years in Stockholm in June — ending a run of 40 straight victories — and then retired from the London Diamond League in July with a groin twinge, showed that he was back in top form.
结构分析：主句主语是"Duplantis"，谓语是"showed"，宾语是"that he was back in top form"（that引导的宾语从句）。在主语和谓语之间，"who had been beaten for the first time in three years in Stockholm in June"是who引导的非限制性定语从句修饰Duplantis；破折号中间的"ending a run of 40 straight victories"是现在分词短语作伴随状语，补充说明被击败的结果；"and then retired from the London Diamond League in July with a groin twinge"与had been beaten并列，同为定语从句的谓语。此句通过定语从句交代了杜普兰蒂斯近期经历的挫折，突出了他在洛桑的回归。
翻译：杜普兰蒂斯在6月的斯德哥尔摩三年来首次被击败——终结了40连胜——随后又在7月的伦敦钻石联赛中因腹股沟不适退赛，但他在这场比赛中展现了自己已重回巅峰状态。
③ Karalis had two attempts at that height before making the bold decision to pass his final attempt and raise the bar directly to 6.26m — what would have been a nine-centimetre personal best.
结构分析：主句主语是"Karalis"，谓语是"had"，宾语是"two attempts"，"at that height"是介词短语作定语；"before making the bold decision"是介词短语作时间状语，before后接动名词making；"to pass his final attempt and raise the bar directly to 6.26m"是不定式短语作decision的后置定语，其中pass和raise并列；破折号后"what would have been a nine-centimetre personal best"是what引导的从句作6.26m的同位语，使用虚拟语气"would have been"表示与事实相反的假设（实际上没有跳过）。
翻译：卡里利斯在该高度试跳两次后，做出了大胆决定——放弃最后一次试跳，将横杆直接升至6.26米，这将是他九厘米的个人最好成绩。
④ Sport, at its finest, is not merely about winning but about the human spirit's relentless drive to reach higher — one centimetre at a time.
结构分析：主句主语是"Sport"，谓语是"is"；"at its finest"是介词短语作插入语，意为"在其最佳状态时"；"not merely about winning but about..."是not merely...but...结构，意为"不仅仅是关于……而是关于……"，about后接动名词winning和名词短语the human spirit's relentless drive；"to reach higher"是不定式短语作drive的后置定语；句末破折号后"one centimetre at a time"是补充说明，意为"每次一厘米"，呼应撑杆跳每次升高一厘米的特点。
翻译：体育，在其最高境界上，不仅仅是关于胜利，而是关于人类精神不懈追求更高境界的动力——每次一厘米。
