Chinese Team Wins European Inventor Award 2026 for Revolutionary Battery Recycling Technology

科技类 · 新能源技术
Date: August 2, 2026 | Source: Xinhua News Agency

【文章摘要】

2026年7月，中国广东邦普循环科技有限公司的余海军和谢英豪团队凭借创新的电池回收技术，在德国柏林荣获欧洲发明家奖两项大奖。他们开发的"逆向产品定位设计"技术能将废旧锂电池高效转化为高性能正极材料，为全球电动汽车产业的可持续发展提供了重要解决方案。

A Historic Achievement in Green Technology

In a landmark moment for China's renewable energy sector, a Chinese research team has made history by winning two prestigious awards at the 2026 European Inventor Award ceremony held in Berlin, Germany. Yu Haijun and Xie Yinghao, researchers from Guangdong Brunp Recycling Technology Co., Ltd., received both the "Non-European Patent Organization Member Country Award" and the "Popular Award" for their groundbreaking work in battery recycling technology.

The European Inventor Award, established by the European Patent Office, is one of the most distinguished honors in the global innovation landscape. This year's ceremony highlighted breakthrough contributions addressing some of humanity's most pressing challenges, particularly in energy, environmental technology, and sustainable development. The fact that a Chinese team secured two awards simultaneously marks a significant milestone in international recognition of China's technological innovation capabilities.

The Revolutionary "Reverse Product Positioning Design"

At the heart of this achievement lies an innovative technology known as "reverse product positioning design." This sophisticated approach enables the direct transformation of spent lithium-ion batteries into high-performance cathode materials suitable for manufacturing new batteries. The technology achieves remarkable efficiency in recovering critical materials including nickel, cobalt, manganese, and lithium—elements that are essential for electric vehicle battery production.

Traditional battery recycling methods often involve complex chemical processes that consume significant energy and may result in material loss. However, this new technology represents a paradigm shift by establishing a more direct pathway from waste to valuable resources. According to the European Patent Office, the innovation offers a sustainable solution to the growing challenge of battery waste management while simultaneously addressing the global demand for critical battery materials.

Addressing a Global Challenge

With the rapid expansion of electric vehicles and energy storage systems worldwide, the demand for battery materials has surged dramatically. The European Patent Office noted in its announcement that as electric vehicles and energy storage technologies accelerate their deployment globally, the demand for critical battery materials has increased substantially. Consequently, spent battery recycling has emerged as a crucial link in the sustainable development chain.

Statistics reveal that China has become the world's largest electric vehicle market, with millions of EVs on the road. As these vehicles age, the question of what happens to their batteries becomes increasingly urgent. This award-winning technology provides an answer that benefits not only China but the entire global community working toward carbon neutrality goals.

Implications for Sustainable Development

The recognition at the European Inventor Award underscores the growing importance of circular economy principles in modern technology development. By enabling efficient recovery and reuse of battery materials, this technology contributes to reducing dependence on newly mined resources, minimizing environmental impact, and promoting sustainable industrial practices.

In his acceptance remarks, Yu Haijun emphasized that the award represents tremendous recognition of their smart battery recycling technology. He expressed hope that the technology would be widely applied to advance sustainable energy solutions globally. This sentiment reflects the broader vision of using innovation to address shared global challenges.

Looking Ahead

The achievement of the Brunp Recycling team signals a new era in battery technology development. As nations worldwide accelerate their transition to clean energy, technologies that enable sustainable resource management will become increasingly valuable. This award not only recognizes past achievements but also highlights the pathway forward for responsible technological advancement.

Industry experts suggest that such innovations could fundamentally reshape the electric vehicle industry's supply chain, making it more resilient and environmentally friendly. As more countries implement regulations requiring battery recycling and manufacturers increasingly prioritize sustainability, technologies like the one developed by the Chinese team will play a pivotal role in shaping a greener future.

🔍 长难句分析 (Complex Sentence Analysis)

1. Yu Haijun and Xie Yinghao, researchers from Guangdong Brunp Recycling Technology Co., Ltd., received both the "Non-European Patent Organization Member Country Award" and the "Popular Award" for their groundbreaking work in battery recycling technology.

【结构分析】主语：Yu Haijun and Xie Yinghao；同位语：researchers from...（介绍主语身份）；谓语：received；宾语：both...and...连接的两个奖项；状语：for their groundbreaking work...（获奖原因）

【参考译文】来自广东邦普循环科技有限公司的研究人员余海军和谢英豪凭借其在电池回收技术方面的开创性工作，同时获得了"非欧洲专利组织成员国奖"和"人气奖"。

2. This sophisticated approach enables the direct transformation of spent lithium-ion batteries into high-performance cathode materials suitable for manufacturing new batteries.

【结构分析】主语：This sophisticated approach；谓语：enables；宾语：the direct transformation...；其中spent lithium-ion batteries是"废旧锂电池"，high-performance cathode materials是"高性能正极材料"，suitable for...是形容词短语后置修饰materials

【参考译文】这种先进的方法能够将废旧锂电池直接转化为适用于制造新电池的高性能正极材料。

3. The European Patent Office noted in its announcement that as electric vehicles and energy storage technologies accelerate their deployment globally, the demand for critical battery materials has increased substantially.

【结构分析】主句：The European Patent Office noted...；宾语从句：that...（that引导整个从句）；在宾语从句中，as引导时间状语从句，主句是the demand...has increased substantially

【参考译文】欧洲专利局在公告中指出，随着电动汽车和储能技术在全球范围内加速推广，对关键电池材料的需求大幅增长。

4. By enabling efficient recovery and reuse of battery materials, this technology contributes to reducing dependence on newly mined resources, minimizing environmental impact, and promoting sustainable industrial practices.

【结构分析】By enabling...是方式状语；主语：this technology；谓语：contributes to；宾语是三个动名词短语并列：reducing...，minimizing...，and promoting...

【参考译文】通过实现电池材料的高效回收和再利用，这项技术有助于减少对新开采资源的依赖、最小化环境影响，并促进可持续工业实践。
