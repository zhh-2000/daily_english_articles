World's First Zero-Carbon Airport Opens in Ordos, China

📅 July 27, 2026 | 📂 Science & Technology | ⏱️ Reading Time: 8 mins
新能源 / New Energy

📝 文章摘要

2026年7月25日，全球首个零碳机场技术成果在内蒙古鄂尔多斯伊金霍洛国际机场正式发布。该系统采用创新的"风-光-储-冷-热-电"零碳能源体系，基于二氧化碳冷热一体化技术，使机场清洁能源占比超过90%，实现整体净碳排放量≤0。这一技术突破不仅为民航业绿色发展树立了标杆，也为全球碳中和目标提供了创新解决方案。

In a groundbreaking achievement for sustainable aviation, Ordos Yijinhuoluo International Airport in Inner Mongolia has become the world's first zero-carbon airport, with its technology results officially released on July 25, 2026. This milestone marks a significant step forward in China's pursuit of carbon neutrality goals and demonstrates how renewable energy innovation can transform traditional infrastructure.

The zero-carbon airport system, developed by researchers from Peking University Ordos Energy Research Institute, utilizes an innovative "wind-solar-storage-cooling-heating-electricity" integrated energy framework. Unlike conventional airports that rely heavily on fossil fuels, this system harnesses the region's abundant natural resources—approximately 2,800 hours of annual sunshine and strong wind reserves—to generate clean electricity while maintaining stable operations throughout the year.

What distinguishes this technology is its pioneering use of transcritical carbon dioxide thermodynamic cycles for power generation and heating. By replacing traditional fossil fuel systems with this advanced carbon dioxide-based technology, the airport has eliminated the need for natural gas and fluorocarbon-based refrigerants. According to the research team, the system achieves zero fossil fuel consumption, zero fluorocarbon emissions, and zero cooling water usage, representing a comprehensive approach to green energy supply.

The results have been impressive: over 90% of the airport's energy now comes from clean sources, with annual wind and solar power generation exceeding 21 million kilowatt-hours. Heating and cooling energy consumption has dropped by 60%, while operational costs have fallen by half. Each year, the system saves more than 2 million cubic meters of natural gas, significantly reducing both carbon emissions and operational expenses.

Beyond aviation, this zero-carbon technology has broader applications. The research team indicated that the system can be adapted for industrial parks, computing centers, and manufacturing facilities, offering a scalable solution for various sectors seeking to reduce their carbon footprint. As countries worldwide intensify their efforts against climate change, such innovations provide practical pathways toward sustainable development.

Certified by the China Quality Certification Center and China Communications and Transportation Association, the Ordos zero-carbon airport technology has been assessed as reaching international leading levels. Industry experts believe this achievement will serve as a model for airports worldwide and accelerate the global transition toward green aviation infrastructure, contributing meaningfully to international climate commitments.

As the aviation industry faces growing pressure to address its environmental impact, innovations like the Ordos zero-carbon airport demonstrate that sustainable solutions are not merely theoretical concepts but practical realities. With continued investment in research and development, such technologies promise to reshape how we think about transportation infrastructure in an era defined by climate consciousness.

🔬 长难句解析

句子 1

"Unlike conventional airports that rely heavily on fossil fuels, this system harnesses the region's abundant natural resources—approximately 2,800 hours of annual sunshine and strong wind reserves—to generate clean electricity while maintaining stable operations throughout the year."

结构分析：
• 主句：this system harnesses...natural resources...to generate clean electricity
• 状语：Unlike conventional airports（介词短语作状语）
• 定语从句：that rely heavily on fossil fuels（修饰 airports）
• 破折号内容：approximately 2,800 hours...（解释说明 natural resources）
• 结果状语：to generate clean electricity
• 伴随状语：while maintaining stable operations

参考译文：与传统严重依赖化石燃料的机场不同，该系统利用该地区丰富的自然资源——每年约2800小时的日照和充沛的风力储备——来产生清洁电力，同时保持全年的稳定运营。

句子 2

"By replacing traditional fossil fuel systems with this advanced carbon dioxide-based technology, the airport has eliminated the need for natural gas and fluorocarbon-based refrigerants."

结构分析：
• 方式状语：By replacing...with...（通过用...替代...）
• 主句：the airport has eliminated the need for...
• 宾语：the need for natural gas and fluorocarbon-based refrigerants

参考译文：通过用这种先进的二氧化碳基技术替代传统化石燃料系统，该机场消除了对天然气和氟利昂制冷剂的需求。

句子 3

"As countries worldwide intensify their efforts against climate change, such innovations provide practical pathways toward sustainable development."

结构分析：
• 时间状语从句：As countries worldwide intensify their efforts...
• 主句：such innovations provide practical pathways...
• 介词短语：toward sustainable development（修饰 pathways）

参考译文：随着世界各国加大应对气候变化的力度，此类创新为可持续发展提供了切实可行的途径。

句子 4

"Certified by the China Quality Certification Center and China Communications and Transportation Association, the Ordos zero-carbon airport technology has been assessed as reaching international leading levels."

结构分析：
• 过去分词短语作状语：Certified by...（被...认证）
• 主句：the Ordos zero-carbon airport technology has been assessed as...
• 宾补：reaching international leading levels

参考译文：经中国质量认证中心和中国交通运输协会认证，鄂尔多斯零碳机场技术被评估为达到国际领先水平。
