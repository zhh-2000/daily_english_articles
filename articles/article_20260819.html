Robotaxis Hit the Streets: Autonomous Driving Enters a New Era

📅 August 19, 2026  |  📖 约950词  |  ⏱️ 建议阅读时间：15分钟

📋 文章摘要

2026年8月，自动驾驶行业迎来里程碑：Waymo获准在加州18县运营无人出租车，Amazon旗下Zoox在拉斯维加斯启动首个商业付费服务，Uber投入超100亿美元构建robotaxi聚合平台。这些突破标志着无人驾驶从测试阶段正式迈入商业化时代，同时也引发了关于安全监管和技术路线的持续讨论。

🚗 A Milestone Week for Autonomous Driving

In August 2026, the autonomous driving industry experienced what many observers are calling a watershed moment. Within a single week, three major developments reshaped the landscape of self-driving transportation, signaling that robotaxis are no longer experimental projects but emerging commercial services available to ordinary citizens.

On August 14, Waymo—the autonomous driving subsidiary of Alphabet—received formal approval from the California Public Utilities Commission (CPUC) to operate fully driverless, paid robotaxi rides across 18 counties. The permit covers the Bay Area, Los Angeles, Sacramento, and San Diego, allowing Waymo's fleet of approximately 3,000 vehicles to operate on highways, city streets, and parking lots. The company's robotaxis have already driven roughly 220 million autonomous miles and completed over 20 million trips, establishing a substantial safety record.

🏪 Amazon's Zoox Launches Commercial Service

Just days earlier, Amazon's Zoox began charging passengers for driverless robotaxi rides in Las Vegas starting August 10. This marked Zoox's first commercial service, coming shortly after the company became the first autonomous ride-hailing operator to receive an exemption from the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) from federal rules requiring human driving controls. The approval allows Zoox to deploy up to 2,500 vehicles in each of the next two years.

Zoox's vehicles are purpose-built robotaxis, designed from the ground up without steering wheels or pedals—a radical departure from conventional cars adapted for autonomous driving. The company said fares would be comparable to the "comfort" tier offered by traditional ride-hailing companies, with pricing based on a base fare plus time and distance. Notably, riders will not pay more if the robotaxi takes a longer route than originally planned.

💰 Uber's $10 Billion Bet

Meanwhile, Uber Technologies announced a commitment of over $10 billion to fund its autonomous vehicle aggregator strategy. The massive capital allocation, backed by $10.1 billion in trailing twelve-month free cash flow, aims to expand commercial deployments to 15 cities by late 2026 and 28 by 2028. Uber has established commercial ties or equity positions with over 30 global autonomous vehicle developers.

This strategy reflects a shift in Uber's approach. As its anchor partner Waymo prepares to exit exclusive distribution arrangements to focus on direct-to-consumer operations, Uber is hedging its bets by partnering with multiple AV developers. Major commitments include a $500 million investment in Lucid to procure 35,000 Nuro-equipped robotaxis and a $1.25 billion deal with Rivian for autonomous platforms.

🌍 Global Expansion

The robotaxi revolution extends far beyond the United States. In London, Transport for London (TfL) issued licenses for Wayve's fleet of autonomous Ford Mustang Mach-E vehicles operating on the Uber platform. The deployment features Wayve's AV2.0 platform, an end-to-end embodied AI driving architecture that bypasses HD mapping in favor of neural-network-based real-time perception. Uber has already attracted over 100,000 waitlist registrants for early trial access.

In China, companies like Pony.ai and WeRide are accelerating their own commercial deployment roadmaps. WeRide has expanded its European footprint into Denmark through a partnership with GreenMobility, while Pony.ai plans to deploy 500 to 1,000 autonomous heavy-duty trucks over the next two to three years. Chinese autonomous vehicle technology is now operating across 40 cities worldwide.

⚠️ Safety and Regulatory Challenges

Despite the enthusiasm, robotaxi operators continue to face significant regulatory scrutiny over safety. Waymo experienced a June recall after vehicles entered closed freeway construction zones, while Zoox recalled vehicles in July after one of its robotaxis struggled to detect heavy smoke at an emergency scene. NHTSA has imposed additional reporting requirements covering crashes and vehicles stopping inappropriately on roads.

Waymo Co-CEO Dmitri Dolgov addressed the technical debate by outlining the limitations of vision-only perception architectures for Level 4 autonomous driving. He argued that camera-based sensing hits an early performance plateau, and achieving the safety thresholds required for driver removal necessitates multi-modal sensor fusion integrating high-resolution cameras, 3D LiDAR, and long-range radar. This positions Waymo's multi-sensor approach in direct contrast to Tesla's vision-only philosophy.

As autonomous driving transitions from testing to commercial reality, the competition among technology companies, the evolution of regulatory frameworks, and the resolution of remaining safety challenges will collectively determine how quickly self-driving vehicles reshape urban transportation in the years ahead.

🔍 长难句解析

① On August 14, Waymo—the autonomous driving subsidiary of Alphabet—received formal approval from the California Public Utilities Commission (CPUC) to operate fully driverless, paid robotaxi rides across 18 counties.
结构分析：主句主语是"Waymo"，谓语是"received"，宾语是"formal approval"。句首"On August 14"是时间状语；两个破折号之间是同位语，补充说明Waymo的身份；"from the CPUC"是介词短语作状语表示来源；"to operate..."是不定式短语作目的状语，其中"fully driverless, paid"是并列形容词修饰robotaxi rides。
翻译：8月14日，Waymo——Alphabet旗下的自动驾驶子公司——获得了加州公共事业委员会（CPUC）的正式批准，可以在18个县运营完全无人驾驶的付费出租车服务。
② This marked Zoox's first commercial service, coming shortly after the company became the first autonomous ride-hailing operator to receive an exemption from the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) from federal rules requiring human driving controls.
结构分析：主句是"This marked Zoox's first commercial service"。"coming shortly after..."是现在分词短语作伴随状语；在after引导的时间状语从句中，"the company"是主语，"became"是谓语，"the first...operator"是表语；其后"to receive an exemption"是不定式短语作定语修饰operator；"from NHTSA"和"from federal rules"两个介词短语修饰exemption；"requiring human driving controls"是现在分词短语作后置定语修饰federal rules。
翻译：这标志着Zoox的首个商业服务，此前不久，该公司刚刚成为第一家获得美国国家公路交通安全管理局（NHTSA）联邦法规豁免的自动驾驶网约车运营商，该法规要求配备人类驾驶控制装置。
③ Uber has established commercial ties or equity positions with over 30 global autonomous vehicle developers, OEM hardware partners, and freight entities.
结构分析：主句主语是"Uber"，谓语是"has established"（现在完成时），宾语是"commercial ties or equity positions"。"with over 30 global..."是介词短语作状语，其中三个并列名词短语autonomous vehicle developers, OEM hardware partners, and freight entities共同修饰30 global后面的内容，表示合作对象的多样性。
翻译：Uber已与全球30多家自动驾驶开发商、OEM硬件合作伙伴和货运企业建立了商业合作或股权关系。
④ He argued that camera-based sensing hits an early performance plateau, and achieving the safety thresholds required for driver removal necessitates multi-modal sensor fusion integrating high-resolution cameras, 3D LiDAR, and long-range radar.
结构分析：主句是"He argued that..."，that引导宾语从句。宾语从句由and连接两个并列分句：第一个分句主语是"camera-based sensing"，谓语是"hits"，宾语是"plateau"；第二个分句主语是"achieving the safety thresholds"（动名词短语作主语），谓语是"necessitates"，宾语是"multi-modal sensor fusion"。"required for driver removal"是过去分词短语作后置定语修饰thresholds；"integrating..."是现在分词短语作后置定语修饰fusion。
翻译：他认为基于摄像头的感知会早早遇到性能瓶颈，而达到移除驾驶员所需的安全门槛，需要整合高分辨率摄像头、3D激光雷达和远程雷达的多模态传感器融合。
