NUS Engineers Colour-Sensing Yeast for Programmable Biomanufacturing

📅 August 28, 2026  |  📖 约960词  |  ⏱️ 建议阅读时间：15分钟

📋 文章摘要

2026年8月11日，新加坡国立大学（NUS）宣布一项合成生物学重大突破：研究团队首次将同一种酵母菌株改造为能同时响应红光和蓝光两种颜色的生物。团队开发出名为y-iLight的单一红光感应蛋白，并通过模块化蛋白质工程解决了蓝光串扰难题，实现了两个基因表达通道的独立控制。利用这一双通道系统，研究人员通过调控红蓝光的比例与时机来控制酵母生产木犀草素（luteolin），并用红光触发酵母絮凝沉降，实现生产与分离的一体化。该成果于2026年5月22日发表在《自然·通讯》杂志，为可编程生物制造、代谢通路优化和活体材料开辟了新方向。

🧪 From Bread to Biotechnology: Yeast Gets a New Job

Baker's yeast, scientifically known as Saccharomyces cerevisiae, is one of biotechnology's most important microorganisms. For thousands of years, humans have relied on it to leaven bread and ferment beer. More recently, advances in synthetic biology — a field that designs and builds new biological parts, devices and systems — have enabled engineered yeast to convert sugar into a wide range of products, including medicines, fuels and industrial chemicals. Yet even well-engineered yeast cells can be difficult to control in a precise and timely way. Traditionally, scientists have relied on adding chemical inducers to switch genes on or off, a process that is both costly and difficult to reverse once started.

A team of researchers from the National University of Singapore (NUS), led by Associate Professor Poh Chueh Loo from NUS Synthetic Biology for Clinical and Technological Innovation (SynCTI) and the Department of Biomedical Engineering under the College of Design and Engineering (CDE), addressed this long-standing challenge using a powerful approach called optogenetics. Optogenetics is a strategy that rewires cells so that selected genes turn on or off in response to light. By exposing yeast to different colours and patterns of light, the team showed that biological instructions could be delivered dynamically, without repeatedly adding chemical inducers.

🌈 Life in Technicolour: Seeing Red and Blue

While yeast had been previously engineered to respond to single colours of light, this is the first time that a single strain of yeast has been engineered to respond to more than one colour. The NUS team published their research findings in the scientific journal Nature Communications on 22 May 2026.

A key challenge was to make yeast respond reliably to red light. Existing red light-responsive optogenetic systems in yeast require several introduced genes, additional cofactors — also known as "helper" molecules — or careful handling to avoid unintended activation. These limitations make them difficult to combine with other light-controlled systems. The team adapted a light-sensitive tool previously used in bacteria and mammalian cells into a single red light-responsive protein for yeast, which they named y-iLight. When exposed to red light, y-iLight attaches to certain DNA sequences in the yeast and turns on specific genes. Importantly, y-iLight works without the need for additional chemicals beyond those naturally found in yeast, making it simpler, more cost-effective, and more reliable for practical use.

🔧 Solving the Crosstalk Puzzle

Although y-iLight was an important improvement, it initially had a serious limitation: blue light could also activate it. This crosstalk meant that the red light system could not yet operate independently alongside a blue light system. To solve this, the researchers used a modular protein-engineering strategy. They fused y-iLight to protein modules designed to block its activity specifically under blue light, then tested different combinations to identify variants that preserved red light responsiveness while suppressing unwanted blue light activation.

When the improved red light system was combined with an established blue light-responsive system called EL222, the researchers achieved independent control of two gene-expression channels in the same yeast strain. This represents a major step toward multiplexed optogenetics in yeast. As Linus Tan Yu Han, a PhD student at NUS CDE and lead author of the paper, explained, "Achieving this level of independent control without the two systems interfering with each other was a major hurdle in yeast. By solving the blue-light crosstalk issue, we opened the door to much more complex genetic programming in yeast using colour."

🏭 Colourful Instructions for Cell Factories

With a dual-channel optogenetic yeast strain in hand, the team demonstrated how multi-coloured light could deliver more complex instructions. They placed two enzymes involved in producing luteolin — a natural plant chemical with potential health-related applications — under separate red and blue light control. By exposing the yeast to different proportions and timing of red and blue light, the researchers could adjust how the yeast made luteolin. This light-based control also helped researchers understand how the process worked. In particular, they discovered that one enzyme, called F3′H, was less effective in the later stages of culture, providing useful insight on how to improve the process in the future.

The team also used optogenetics to control how yeast cells behave. By linking the flocculation gene FLO1, which makes yeast cells stick together, to a red light-activated genetic switch, they showed that red light could trigger yeast cells to clump together and sink. In their experiment, yeast first produced luteolin under blue light, then clumped together under red light. This demonstrates how light can control both production and separation, which could inspire cleaner and more programmable biomanufacturing strategies.

🎨 Painting with Living Light

Optogenetics also offers spatial control: genes can be switched on only where light is projected. To demonstrate this, the NUS team engineered yeast to produce different coloured compounds in response to red or blue light, spread the cells as a thin layer on agar, and projected light through masks. The result was a set of patterned, multi-colour "living images" grown by yeast — a striking visual proof that biology can be programmed as precisely as a computer-controlled printer.

"Together, the work shows that yeast can be programmed with multiple colours of light to control gene expression, metabolic pathways, cell behaviour and spatial patterning," Assoc Prof Poh explained. "The platform could support future applications in biomanufacturing, pathway optimisation and living materials." The research team is currently working on ways to use rationally designed gene networks to boost the properties of these light-sensitive proteins. With increased strength and sensitivity, they believe it will create new opportunities in the way microbes are used to manufacture valuable chemicals and materials — turning the humble yeast that once only leavened bread into a truly programmable microscopic factory.

🔍 长难句解析

① A team of researchers from the National University of Singapore (NUS), led by Associate Professor Poh Chueh Loo from NUS Synthetic Biology for Clinical and Technological Innovation (SynCTI) and the Department of Biomedical Engineering under the College of Design and Engineering (CDE), addressed this long-standing challenge using a powerful approach called optogenetics, a strategy that rewires cells so that selected genes turn on or off in response to light.
结构分析：此句为主从复合句。主句主语是"A team of researchers from the National University of Singapore (NUS)"，其后"led by Associate Professor Poh Chueh Loo from..."是过去分词短语作后置定语（修饰team，表示被动，意为"由……领导的"）；主句谓语是"addressed"，宾语是"this long-standing challenge"，其后"using a powerful approach called optogenetics"是现在分词短语作方式状语（表示主动和方式，意为"使用一种名为光遗传学的强大方法"）；其后的"a strategy that rewires cells so that selected genes turn on or off in response to light"是optogenetics的同位语，其中"that rewires cells"是that引导的限制性定语从句（修饰strategy），"so that selected genes turn on or off in response to light"是so that引导的目的状语从句。该句通过分词定语、分词状语、同位语和定语从句的多层嵌套，将研究团队的身份、方法和原理浓缩在一句话中。
翻译：新加坡国立大学（NUS）的一个研究团队，由来自NUS临床与技术创新合成生物学中心（SynCTI）及设计与工程学院（CDE）生物医学工程系的潘学理副教授领导，利用一种名为光遗传学的强大方法解决了这一长期存在的难题——光遗传学是一种重新连接细胞的策略，使选定的基因能够根据光照开启或关闭。
② They fused y-iLight to protein modules designed to block its activity specifically under blue light, then tested different combinations to identify variants that preserved red light responsiveness while suppressing unwanted blue light activation.
结构分析：此句为并列复合句，由两个并列谓语构成。主语是"They"，第一个谓语是"fused...to..."（将……融合到……），宾语是"y-iLight"和"protein modules"，其中"designed to block its activity specifically under blue light"是过去分词短语作后置定语（修饰protein modules，表示被动，意为"被设计用于在蓝光下特异性阻断其活性的"）；第二个谓语是"tested"，宾语是"different combinations"，其后"to identify variants"是不定式短语作目的状语（意为"以鉴定变体"），"that preserved red light responsiveness while suppressing unwanted blue light activation"是that引导的限制性定语从句（修饰variants），其中"while suppressing unwanted blue light activation"是while引导的现在分词短语作时间/伴随状语（表示同时进行的动作，意为"同时抑制不需要的蓝光激活"）。该句通过两个并列谓语和定语从句的配合，完整描述了研究团队解决串扰问题的实验策略和目标。
翻译：他们将y-iLight与专门设计用于在蓝光下阻断其活性的蛋白质模块融合，然后测试不同组合，以筛选出保留红光响应性同时抑制不必要蓝光激活的变体。
③ When the improved red light system was combined with an established blue light-responsive system called EL222, the researchers achieved independent control of two gene-expression channels in the same yeast strain, which represents a major step toward multiplexed optogenetics in yeast.
结构分析：此句为主从复合句。句首"When the improved red light system was combined with an established blue light-responsive system called EL222"是When引导的时间状语从句，其中"called EL222"是过去分词短语作后置定语（修饰system）；主句主语是"the researchers"，谓语是"achieved"，宾语是"independent control of two gene-expression channels in the same yeast strain"；其后"which represents a major step toward multiplexed optogenetics in yeast"是which引导的非限制性定语从句（修饰整个主句内容，意为"这代表了……"）。该句通过时间状语从句和非限制性定语从句的搭配，说明了实验操作与突破性成果之间的因果逻辑。
翻译：当改进的红光系统与一个名为EL222的成熟蓝光响应系统结合时，研究人员在同一酵母菌株中实现了对两个基因表达通道的独立控制，这代表了向酵母多重光遗传学迈出的重要一步。
④ By linking the flocculation gene FLO1, which makes yeast cells stick together, to a red light-activated genetic switch, they showed that red light could trigger yeast cells to clump together and sink.
结构分析：此句为主从复合句。句首"By linking the flocculation gene FLO1...to a red light-activated genetic switch"是By引导的介词短语作方式状语（By doing...结构，意为"通过将……连接到……"），其中"which makes yeast cells stick together"是which引导的非限制性定语从句（修饰FLO1，插入解释其功能）；主句主语是"they"，谓语是"showed"，其后"that red light could trigger yeast cells to clump together and sink"是that引导的宾语从句，其中"trigger yeast cells to clump together and sink"使用了trigger...to do...结构（意为"促使……做……"）。该句通过介词状语中嵌套非限制性定语从句和宾语从句的运用，清晰地展示了光控基因开关与细胞行为之间的因果关系。
翻译：通过将使酵母细胞聚集的絮凝基因FLO1连接到红光激活的基因开关上，他们表明红光可以促使酵母细胞聚集成团并下沉。
