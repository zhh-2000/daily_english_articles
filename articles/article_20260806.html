Unprecedented Global Engagement

The Milano Cortina 2026 Olympic Winter Games were a resounding global success, reaffirming the enduring strength and universal appeal of the Olympic brand. Independent research shows that the Games were followed by around 2.6 billion people worldwide, setting new benchmarks in fan engagement across multiple platforms.

While linear television remained the dominant viewing platform, the use of digital channels surged to new heights. 17 per cent of viewers watched the Games exclusively through digital platforms, a significant rise from 11 per cent during Beijing 2022. Furthermore, 60 per cent of viewers watched across both television and digital channels, highlighting an increasingly seamless, multi-platform experience for audiences worldwide.

Record-Breaking Digital Presence

Milano Cortina 2026 marked a defining moment in how audiences experience the Olympic Games, with unprecedented levels of digital engagement. The Olympics Web & App reached 133 million cumulative monthly users, nearly double the 68 million recorded in 2022. Social media engagement climbed to 12.7 billion interactions, up dramatically from 3.2 billion during the previous Winter Games.

Global interest in the Olympic Winter Games soared, with social media conversations reaching historic levels. An estimated 110 billion social media engagements were recorded during the Games period globally, while internet search activity reached an index four times higher than that of the previous Olympic Winter Games.

Exceptional Viewer Satisfaction

Viewer satisfaction reached exceptional levels, demonstrating the quality of delivery and atmosphere across host regions. 84 per cent of audiences stated that they enjoyed the Games, a significant increase from 74 per cent previously. Additionally, 53 per cent of viewers expressed a desire to watch more Olympic coverage, up from 46 per cent in previous years.

On television, audiences watched an average of 6.9 hours of Milano Cortina 2026 coverage, compared to 6.1 hours for Beijing 2022. The Games welcomed over 5.4 million spectators and visitors across competition venues, Fan Zones and the Olympic Torch Relay, with 85 per cent of ticketholders rating their experience as good or excellent.

Athletes' Memorable Experience

For athletes, the Games were equally meaningful. Nine in 10 athletes described their participation as one of the most memorable moments of their careers. Among those surveyed, 92 per cent cited competing at the world's biggest multi-sport event as a primary motivation, while 83 per cent highlighted the importance of becoming an Olympian.

IOC President Kirsty Coventry emphasized the unique power of the Olympic Games, stating that billions of people followed the performances and stories of athletes across broadcast, streaming, digital and social media platforms. The incredible global engagement demonstrated that the Olympic Games can unify people and bring them together in a way that few other events can achieve.

Building a Better World Through Sport

At a time of global uncertainty, Milano Cortina 2026 reinforced the unique role of the Olympic Games in bringing people together and inspiring positive change. 79 per cent of respondents considered the Games a success, increasing to 84 per cent in Italy. Furthermore, 74 per cent of Italians believe the Games will leave a lasting positive legacy for the host regions.

The broader significance of the Olympic Movement was clearly recognised by global audiences. 80 per cent agreed that the Olympic Games are more important than ever in a divided world, while 79 per cent said the IOC successfully brings the world together in peaceful competition. Additionally, 77 per cent believe the IOC contributes to building a better world through sport, demonstrating the enduring relevance and impact of Olympic values in contemporary society.
