Chinese Team Sets New World Record in Solar Cell Efficiency

科技类 新能源 2026-08-01

文章摘要

中国科学院化学研究所李永舫院士、孟磊研究员团队在钙钛矿-有机叠层太阳能电池研究领域取得重大突破，认证稳态光电转换效率达到28.04%，刷新该类器件的世界纪录。该研究成果发表于国际学术期刊《自然》，为未来清洁能源应用开辟了新道路。

A research team from the Institute of Chemistry, Chinese Academy of Sciences has achieved a groundbreaking milestone in renewable energy technology. Led by Academician Li Yongfang and Researcher Meng Lei, the team developed a revolutionary perovskite-organic tandem solar cell that has set a new world record with a certified power conversion efficiency of 28.04%.

The Innovation Behind the Breakthrough

Traditional single-junction solar cells, commonly seen in household or power station silicon panels, rely on a single light-absorbing material. This limitation makes it difficult to efficiently utilize photons with different energy levels simultaneously. The Chinese research team's innovative tandem technology stacks two different photovoltaic materials together, enabling layered absorption of sunlight and more comprehensive utilization of solar energy.

The theoretical power conversion efficiency of this new technology is significantly higher than conventional solar cells. Furthermore, the perovskite-organic tandem solar cells possess remarkable characteristics: they are thin, lightweight, and flexible. These properties make them ideal for various applications including building-integrated photovoltaics, portable energy devices, wearable electronics, unmanned aerial vehicles, and space power systems.

Overcoming Technical Challenges

Previous perovskite-organic tandem solar cells suffered from performance degradation and insufficient stability. Addressing these critical issues, the research team introduced a novel additive molecule called TDB. This innovation achieved comprehensive regulation from the preparation stage to the illumination operation stage, effectively resolving the technical shortcomings of such devices.

After third-party certification, the tandem solar cells demonstrated excellent stability. After 625 hours of continuous illumination, the devices still retained 90% of their initial power conversion efficiency. This remarkable stability marks a significant step toward practical applications.

Global Impact and Future Applications

The research findings were published in the prestigious international academic journal Nature on July 13, 2026, representing a major advancement in next-generation solar cell technology. The breakthrough demonstrates China's leadership in renewable energy research and contributes significantly to global sustainable development efforts.

Industry experts predict that this new solar technology will find applications across multiple sectors. From ground-based architecture to transportation systems, from smart wearable devices to satellite power supplies, the technology offers reliable energy solutions for diverse scenarios. The achievement provides new technical support for China's energy structure transformation and sustainable development goals.

As the world confronts climate change challenges, innovations in solar cell technology become increasingly crucial. Higher efficiency means more electricity generated from the same amount of sunlight, reducing the cost of clean energy and accelerating the transition from fossil fuels. The Chinese team's record-breaking achievement represents a significant step toward a sustainable energy future.

长难句解析

1. Led by Academician Li Yongfang and Researcher Meng Lei, the team developed a revolutionary perovskite-organic tandem solar cell that has set a new world record with a certified power conversion efficiency of 28.04%.
句式结构：过去分词短语作状语 + 主句 + that引导定语从句
解析："Led by..."是过去分词短语作状语，表示"在...的带领下"；主句主语是"the team"，谓语是"developed"；that引导定语从句修饰"solar cell"，说明这种太阳能电池取得的成就。
翻译：在李永舫院士和孟磊研究员的带领下，团队开发出了一种革命性的钙钛矿-有机叠层太阳能电池，以28.04%的认证功率转换效率创造了新的世界纪录。
2. The Chinese research team's innovative tandem technology stacks two different photovoltaic materials together, enabling layered absorption of sunlight and more comprehensive utilization of solar energy.
句式结构：主句 + 现在分词短语作结果状语
解析：主句说明技术的核心做法；"enabling..."是现在分词短语作结果状语，表示这项技术带来的结果，其中包含两个并列的动名词短语"layered absorption"和"more comprehensive utilization"。
翻译：中国研究团队创新的叠层技术将两种不同的光伏材料堆叠在一起，从而实现对阳光的分层吸收和对太阳能更全面的利用。
3. Addressing these critical issues, the research team introduced a novel additive molecule called TDB, which achieved comprehensive regulation from the preparation stage to the illumination operation stage, effectively resolving the technical shortcomings of such devices.
句式结构：现在分词短语作状语 + 主句 + which引导非限制性定语从句 + 现在分词短语作结果状语
解析："Addressing..."是现在分词短语作原因状语；主句中"called TDB"是过去分词短语作后置定语；which引导非限制性定语从句修饰"TDB"，说明其作用；"resolving..."是现在分词短语作结果状语。
翻译：为解决这些关键问题，研究团队引入了一种名为TDB的新型添加剂分子，实现了从制备阶段到光照运行阶段的全面调控，有效解决了此类器件的技术缺陷。
4. As the world confronts climate change challenges, innovations in solar cell technology become increasingly crucial, with higher efficiency meaning more electricity generated from the same amount of sunlight, reducing the cost of clean energy and accelerating the transition from fossil fuels.
句式结构：as引导时间状语从句 + 主句 + with复合结构 + 现在分词短语作结果状语
解析：as引导时间状语从句；主句说明创新的重要性；with复合结构作伴随状语，其中"meaning..."是现在分词作定语修饰"efficiency"；"reducing...and accelerating..."是现在分词短语作结果状语。
翻译：随着世界面临气候变化挑战，太阳能电池技术的创新变得愈发重要，更高的效率意味着从等量阳光中产生更多电力，从而降低清洁能源成本，加速从化石燃料的转型。
