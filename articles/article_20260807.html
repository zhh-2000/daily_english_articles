Intangible Cultural Heritage Finds New Life Through Innovation

📅 August 7, 2026 | 📚 Word Count: 950 | ⏱️ Reading Time: 8 min
Category: Culture & Heritage

📝 文章摘要

本文介绍2026年新疆非遗展示活动如何通过创新让传统文化焕发新生。来自全国31个省份的650多项非遗项目和540多位传承人齐聚石河子,展示了皮影戏、桑皮纸、传统刺绣等多种非遗技艺的跨地域融合创新。文章展现了传统文化在现代社会中的传承与发展,以及年轻一代如何通过创意设计让非遗走进当代生活。

A Stage for Cultural Exchange

Under the stage lights in Shihezi, a leather shadow puppet slowly raises an arm, swaying gracefully to the rhythm of melodies unique to the city in northern Xinjiang Uygur autonomous region. Its movements are inspired by a traditional dance of the Uygur ethnic group. The music comes from local performers playing classic Uygur instruments such as the dutar and rawap. Yet, the figure dancing behind the screen belonged to Beijing's centuries-old shadow puppet tradition.

For the performers, it was an opportunity to explore how intangible cultural heritage can travel across regions, connect with new traditions and continue to evolve. The performance was created for the 2026 Xinjiang Is a Nice Place intangible cultural heritage showcase, which opened on Thursday in Shihezi.

Organized by the Ministry of Culture and Tourism, the Xinjiang government, and the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps, the five-day event brought together more than 650 representative intangible cultural heritage practices and over 540 inheritors from 31 provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities, as well as the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps.

Breaking Boundaries Through Collaboration

Rather than simply showcasing traditions from different regions, the event highlighted exchanges and cooperation, encouraging artists and craftspeople to learn from one another and create new forms of expression. For Lu Baogang, an actor with the Beijing shadow puppet troupe, adapting the performance for Xinjiang audiences became the troupe's first challenge.

"When we received the invitation, we were honestly a little nervous," Lu says. "We kept asking ourselves what kind of program Xinjiang audiences would connect with." Instead of bringing an existing production, the troupe chose to weave local cultural elements into a brand-new performance.

The result was a short piece inspired by the story of Wan Tongshu, a cultural worker who contributed to the preservation of Twelve Muqam — a traditional Uygur musical suite inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2008. The Beijing artists created two shadow puppet figures based on Wan and his wife, paying tribute to generations of cultural workers who dedicated themselves to safeguarding Xinjiang's rich musical heritage.

Learning From Each Other

For Lu, the most rewarding part of the project came when the Beijing performers began working closely with musicians from Xinjiang. "At first, communication was not easy because we speak differently," Lu says. "But we share something in common — we are all committed to protecting traditional arts."

After several rehearsals, the two sides gradually found ways to blend the movements of the shadow puppets with the rhythms of Xinjiang's traditional music. Lu says he learned a great deal from the local artists, particularly how to capture the distinctive movements of Xinjiang dance.

"One example is the posture of a Xinjiang woman dancing," he says. "Previously, our puppet simply placed one hand on its waist. The local artists suggested raising the other hand and adding movements that flowed with the rhythm. It instantly made the character feel more natural."

Paper Transformed Into Art

Across the exhibition hall, the Xinjiang mulberry-bark paper, known as sangpi paper, was embarking on a different path toward renewal. Sheets of paper were placed beneath the carved woodblocks used in art studio Rongbaozhai's centuries-old woodblock watermark printing technique, producing a series of collectible artworks.

According to Li Yang, deputy director of Rongbaozhai's woodblock watermark center, each region produces mulberry paper with its own texture and characteristics. For the Xinjiang collaboration, Rongbaozhai used local mulberry paper to print images including the "Five Stars Rising in the East" brocade, discovered at the Niya archaeological site in Hotan prefecture, Xinjiang.

Li believes the paper's distinctive texture could eventually become an artistic advantage. "Its weakness could also become its strength," he says. "In the future, if artists create directly for this material, we may discover a new artistic language."

A New Chapter for Heritage

Cross-regional cooperation from the partnership between Quanzhou in Fujian province and Changji Hui autonomous prefecture in Xinjiang has delivered special performances blending Fujian folk instruments with Xinjiang traditions. The exhibition highlights unique characteristics of different embroidery traditions, including ethnic Kazak and ethnic Hui embroideries, side by side.

For Lu Baogang, the gathering was a reminder that intangible cultural heritage is not confined by geography. "Preserving tradition today is not only about safeguarding old techniques, but also about creating opportunities for different forms of heritage to interact, inspire and evolve," he says.

As China continues to modernize, initiatives like the Xinjiang showcase demonstrate that traditional culture need not be left behind. Through innovation, collaboration and a willingness to embrace change, intangible cultural heritage can find new relevance and reach new audiences in the 21st century.

📖 长难句解析 (Complex Sentences)

① The result was a short piece inspired by the story of Wan Tongshu, a cultural worker who contributed to the preservation of Twelve Muqam — a traditional Uygur musical suite inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2008.
结构分析: 主句是"The result was a short piece",后面是过去分词短语"inspired by..."作定语修饰piece。其中包含定语从句"who contributed to..."修饰Wan Tongshu,以及同位语"a traditional Uygur musical suite..."解释说明Twelve Muqam。
翻译: 成果是一部短片,灵感来自万桐书的故事——他是一位为保护十二木卡姆做出贡献的文化工作者。十二木卡姆是2008年被列入联合国教科文组织人类非物质文化遗产代表作名录的传统维吾尔族音乐套曲。
② For the Xinjiang collaboration, Rongbaozhai used local mulberry paper to print images including the "Five Stars Rising in the East" brocade, discovered at the Niya archaeological site in Hotan prefecture, Xinjiang, as well as a panda-themed design.
结构分析: 主句是"Rongbaozhai used local mulberry paper to print images",宾语images后接现在分词短语"including..."作定语。其中"discovered at..."是过去分词短语作定语修饰brocade。
翻译: 在与新疆的合作中,荣宝斋使用当地桑皮纸印制图像,包括在新疆和田地区尼雅考古遗址发现的"五星出东方"织锦,以及熊猫主题的设计图案。
③ Preserving tradition today is not only about safeguarding old techniques, but also about creating opportunities for different forms of heritage to interact, inspire and evolve.
结构分析: 动名词短语"Preserving tradition today"作主语,谓语是"is"。使用了"not only...but also..."并列结构连接两个介词短语"about safeguarding..."和"about creating..."。不定式"to interact, inspire and evolve"作定语修饰heritage。
翻译: 如今保护传统不仅是要保护古老技艺,还要创造机会让不同形式的遗产相互交流、相互启发、共同发展。
④ As China continues to modernize, initiatives like the Xinjiang showcase demonstrate that traditional culture need not be left behind in the march toward progress.
结构分析: "As China continues to modernize"是时间状语从句。主句主语是initiatives,谓语demonstrate后接that引导的宾语从句。从句中使用"need not be left behind"表示"不必被抛弃"。
翻译: 随着中国继续现代化进程,像新疆展示活动这样的倡议表明,传统文化不必在进步的进程中被抛在身后。
