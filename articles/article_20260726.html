China's Renewable Energy Milestone: Wind and Solar Power Surpass Coal

July 26, 2026 | Source: China Energy Industry Report

📋 文章摘要

中国能源产业迎来历史性时刻！《2026能源产业生态报告》显示，"十四五"期间风电光伏装机首次超越火电，标志着能源结构转型取得重大突破。报告指出，2025年能源重点项目投资超3.5万亿元，新能源产业实现跨越式发展，为全球绿色低碳转型树立典范。本文深入分析中国可再生能源发展的里程碑意义及其全球影响。

A Historic Turning Point

In a landmark achievement for global energy transition, China has officially reached a historic milestone: the combined installed capacity of wind and solar power has surpassed that of coal-fired power plants for the first time. According to the "2026 Energy Industry Ecosystem Report" released on July 25, this transformation occurred during the 14th Five-Year Plan period (2021-2025), marking a fundamental shift in the country's energy landscape.

The report, published by the China Enterprise Reform and Development Society, reveals that China's wind and solar installed capacity grew from 530 million kilowatts in 2020 to 1.84 billion kilowatts by 2025—a nearly 3.5-fold increase in just five years. This unprecedented growth demonstrates China's commitment to achieving carbon neutrality and promoting sustainable development.

Investment Surge and Technological Innovation

The year 2025 witnessed another significant benchmark: national investment in key energy projects exceeded 3.5 trillion yuan for the first time, representing an 11% year-on-year increase. This massive investment has accelerated technological innovation across the renewable energy sector, driving down costs and improving efficiency.

China now leads globally in nuclear power operation capacity, with installed biomass energy generation ranking first worldwide for seven consecutive years. These achievements underscore the nation's comprehensive approach to energy diversification, combining multiple renewable sources to create a robust and sustainable energy ecosystem.

Policy Support and Future Outlook

The National Development and Reform Commission and the National Energy Administration have jointly issued the "15th Five-Year Plan for Renewable Energy Development," outlining ambitious targets for the next phase. Key priorities include accelerating research on perovskite tandem cells and other high-efficiency photovoltaic technologies, exploring space-based solar power generation, and advancing green hydrogen production capabilities.

The plan emphasizes strengthening research on advanced materials, core components, and critical equipment for renewable energy. Innovations in variable-speed, ultra-high-head pumped storage units and giant impulse-type hydropower units for kilometer-level water heads are among the technological breakthroughs targeted for development.

Global Implications

China's renewable energy milestone carries profound implications for global climate action. As the world's largest energy consumer, China's successful transition from fossil fuels to renewable sources provides a powerful model for other nations. The report highlights that China has achieved a dual balance between energy security and green, low-carbon transformation—a challenge many countries continue to face.

International energy experts note that China's experience demonstrates that rapid energy transition is technically feasible and economically viable. The integration of artificial intelligence with energy systems, including innovations like the "Yudian" intelligent simulation model developed by Southern Power Grid, showcases how digital technologies can enhance renewable energy utilization and grid stability.

Local Innovations and Smart Energy Systems

Beyond large-scale infrastructure, China is pioneering innovative energy solutions at the local level. The zero-carbon distributed smart energy center demonstration project in Shaanxi's Yulin Science and Technology Innovation City exemplifies this approach. The facility integrates photovoltaic power generation, water electrolysis for hydrogen production, and fuel cells to achieve zero-carbon electricity, cooling, and heating supply.

Calculations show that this project reduces energy costs by approximately 45% compared to traditional models while eliminating about 8,640 tons of carbon emissions annually. Such demonstration projects provide valuable experience for scaling up integrated renewable energy solutions across urban and rural areas.

🔍 长难句解析

1. According to the "2026 Energy Industry Ecosystem Report" released on July 25, this transformation occurred during the 14th Five-Year Plan period (2021-2025), marking a fundamental shift in the country's energy landscape.
【结构分析】句子主干为"this transformation occurred"，前半部分为介词短语作状语，后半部分"marking..."为现在分词作结果状语。句子展示了报告发布的背景和转型发生的时间及其意义。
2. The report, published by the China Enterprise Reform and Development Society, reveals that China's wind and solar installed capacity grew from 530 million kilowatts in 2020 to 1.84 billion kilowatts by 2025—a nearly 3.5-fold increase in just five years.
【结构分析】主句为"The report reveals that..."，中间插入的过去分词短语"published by..."作定语修饰"report"，that引导宾语从句详细说明增长数据，破折号后为同位语补充说明增长幅度。
3. The National Development and Reform Commission and the National Energy Administration have jointly issued the "15th Five-Year Plan for Renewable Energy Development," outlining ambitious targets for the next phase.
【结构分析】主句结构为"两部门联合发布规划"，现在分词短语"outlining..."作伴随状语，说明规划的主要内容。动词"issued"与"outlining"形成逻辑上的主谓关系。
4. International energy experts note that China's experience demonstrates that rapid energy transition is technically feasible and economically viable, providing a powerful model for other nations.
【结构分析】主句为"experts note that..."，第一个that引导宾语从句，其中包含第二个that引导的宾语从句作"demonstrates"的宾语，句末现在分词"providing"作结果状语。
