Global Battery Storage: Powering the Clean Energy Revolution

August 10, 2026 | Technology & Environment | Word Count: 920

今日最新

文章摘要

本文介绍了2026年8月全球电池储能领域的最新发展。从印度的20 GWh大规模储能计划到美国的10亿美元融资，从德国首个无补贴光储混合项目到日本的电网级电池开发，各国正在加速布局清洁能源基础设施。电池储能技术不仅解决了可再生能源的间歇性问题，也为实现碳中和目标提供了关键技术支撑。

The global transition to clean energy is accelerating, and battery storage technology has emerged as a crucial enabler of this transformation. On August 10, 2026, multiple countries announced significant developments in the battery energy storage systems (BESS) sector, highlighting the growing importance of this technology in achieving climate goals.

India's Ambitious Storage Initiative

Indian electric vehicle manufacturer Ola Electric has entered a memorandum of understanding (MoU) with Axis Energy Ventures to deploy up to 20 GWh of battery energy storage systems by 2032. This partnership marks Ola Electric's expansion into the utility-scale storage market through its new platform, Ola Mahashakti. The initiative is designed to support commercial, industrial, and utility-scale applications, with all systems manufactured domestically in India. Axis Energy, which has secured grid approvals for over 3.75 GW of projects in Andhra Pradesh and Rajasthan, will integrate these batteries into its renewable energy pipeline.

United States: Massive Investment in Home Batteries

In the United States, Base Power has raised $1 billion in Series D funding, reaching a post-money valuation of $13 billion. The company, founded in 2023, has now raised more than $2.5 billion to date. The latest funding round was led by Ribbit, Addition, Valor Equity Partners, and JPMorganChase's Strategic Investment Group. Alongside this financing, Base has launched Base Core, a home battery produced at its factory in Austin, Texas. Available in 39.2 kWh and 78.4 kWh configurations, the battery is designed and built entirely in the US and can be installed in under an hour, providing extended backup power while supporting grid stability.

Germany's Innovative Hybrid Project

German renewable energy developer MaxSolar GmbH and DB Energie have signed a 10-year hybrid power purchase agreement (PPA) for the Friedrichshof solar-plus-storage project in Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania. This project combines a 37 MW ground-mounted solar plant with a 15 MW/30 MWh co-located BESS and is described as Germany's first subsidy-free project to jointly market solar generation and battery storage under a hybrid PPA. The facility is scheduled for commissioning in summer 2027, with the solar plant expected to generate approximately 36,680 MWh annually. DB Energie, a subsidiary of German railway group Deutsche Bahn, will use the contracted renewable electricity as part of its railway power portfolio.

Scotland's Growing Storage Portfolio

Carlyle-backed Revera Energy has begun construction of the 200 MW/400 MWh Windyhill BESS project in Bearsden, Scotland. This project is the first of three battery projects in Revera's Scottish portfolio to enter construction, forming part of a planned nearly 1 GW/2 GWh portfolio representing more than £500 million in investment. GE Vernova serves as the technology partner, while Mitie and OCU support project delivery. Commercial operations are expected to begin in Q1 2028, supporting Scotland's transition to a more flexible, renewables-driven grid.

Japan's Grid-Scale Development

Japan's Intellect has started development of a grid-scale battery storage project in Kumamoto City. The project features a rated output of about 2 MW and storage capacity of 8 MWh, developed on land owned by the BLD Group. Operations are expected to begin in June 2027, with Intellect handling project development, EPC, and O&M, while NAXT provides asset management and an aggregator manages market operations and power balancing. This project will support electricity supply-demand balancing and grid stability in the region.

These global developments demonstrate that battery storage is no longer a peripheral technology but a central pillar of the clean energy transition. As countries worldwide invest billions of dollars in storage infrastructure, the technology is becoming increasingly cost-effective and scalable, paving the way for a renewable-powered future.

长难句解析

Indian electric vehicle manufacturer Ola Electric has entered a memorandum of understanding (MoU) with Axis Energy Ventures to explore the deployment of up to 20 GWh of battery energy storage systems (BESS) by 2032, which is the first large-scale partnership for Ola Electric's upcoming energy storage platform for commercial, industrial, and utility-scale applications.

解析：主句为"Ola Electric has entered a MoU with Axis Energy Ventures"，不定式短语"to explore..."表目的，which引导的非限制性定语从句修饰整个主句内容。
翻译：印度电动汽车制造商Ola Electric已与Axis Energy Ventures签署谅解备忘录，探索到2032年部署高达20 GWh电池储能系统，这是Ola Electric即将推出的商用、工业和公用事业规模储能平台的首次大规模合作。

German renewable energy developer MaxSolar GmbH and DB Energie have signed a 10-year hybrid power purchase agreement (PPA) for the Friedrichshof solar-plus-storage project in Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania, which combines a 37 MW ground-mounted solar plant with a 15 MW/30 MWh co-located BESS and is described as Germany's first subsidy-free project to jointly market solar generation and battery storage under a hybrid PPA.

句型结构：主句(S + V + O) + 介词短语作定语 + which引导的定语从句
解析：主句为"MaxSolar GmbH and DB Energie have signed a 10-year hybrid PPA"，介词短语"for the Friedrichshof..."作定语修饰agreement，which引导定语从句修饰project，从句中包含两个并列的谓语"combines"和"is described"。
翻译：德国可再生能源开发商MaxSolar GmbH与DB Energie为位于梅克伦堡-前波美拉尼亚的Friedrichshof光储项目签署了为期10年的混合购电协议，该项目结合了37兆瓦地面光伏电站和15兆瓦/30兆瓦时共址储能系统，被称为德国首个在混合购电协议下共同销售光伏发电和电池储能的无补贴项目。
