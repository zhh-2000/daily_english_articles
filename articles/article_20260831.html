The Light: Durham's New Museum Illuminates Where Art Meets Innovation

📅 August 31, 2026  |  📖 约980词  |  ⏱️ 建议阅读时间：15分钟

📋 文章摘要

2026年8月22日，英国达勒姆全新文化场馆"The Light"正式开幕，首展"太阳的胜利：与光的邂逅"汇集了Olafur Eliasson、Tracey Emin、Jenny Holzer等国际知名艺术家的作品。场馆由达勒姆步兵博物馆改建而成，以出生于此地的灯泡发明人Joseph Swan为灵感，探索光作为艺术媒介从荧光灯到LED再到数字媒体的演变。展览围绕感知、文本、绘画之光和科学技术五大主题展开，融合当代艺术与本地历史、科学发现和技术创新，成为达勒姆创新区的创意枢纽。

🏛️ A New Cultural Landmark

August 2026 marks the opening of The Light in Durham, England — formerly the Durham Light Infantry (DLI) Museum and Art Gallery — following a significant investment by Durham County Council and a transformation by award-winning architects NORR. This new cultural venue brings together contemporary art and creativity with local history, scientific discovery and technological innovation. It is the first venue in County Durham of its scale and remit dedicated to delivering contemporary art of national and international significance.

The venue's links to military history have been carefully retained through a dedicated DLI Gallery that tells the story of the historic regiment through objects, documents and film footage from the DLI Collection. Going beyond military history, the DLI exhibition features images, sound, hands-on interactive displays and rich sensory experiences which tell a deeply human story of identity, belonging and community. The new exhibition galleries, positioned on the top floor of the building, have been designed to host world-class national and touring exhibitions.

💡 Inspired by a Local Inventor

The name of the venue reflects County Durham's many associations with light. Joseph Swan (1828–1914), a British physicist, chemist and inventor of the incandescent light bulb, was born in Sunderland within the historic county of Durham. His spirit of innovation continues in the region today with world-class research into space, optics and communication at Durham University and in the emerging Durham Innovation District, of which The Light will play a key role as a meeting place and creative hub.

County Durham's connections to light stretch even further back in time — from the creation of Bede's lunar calendar 1,300 years ago to Durham University's involvement in the NASA James Webb Space Telescope. The venue thus bridges centuries of scientific curiosity and artistic expression, positioning itself as both a cultural destination and a symbol of regional ambition.

🎨 Victory Over the Sun: Encounters with Light

The Light opened on 22 August with Victory Over the Sun: Encounters with Light, a pivotal exhibition exploring artists using light in extraordinary and experimental ways. The show examines how artists have employed light as a material in their work from the 1960s to the present day, using newly developed technologies ranging from fluorescent tubes and neons to LEDs and digital media. This familiar medium is explored in different and complex ways, from single artworks to immersive installations, throughout the building.

The exhibition includes new commissions and works by renowned international artists, among them Olafur Eliasson, Tracey Emin, Jenny Holzer, David Shrigley, Steve McQueen, Cerith Wyn Evans and Chila Burman. It is organised around five core themes: artworks using light sources, perception, text, drawing on light, and science and technology. Each section reveals a different facet of how light can be manipulated, perceived and interpreted as both a physical phenomenon and an artistic medium.

🔬 Five Themes of Illumination

Swan's Practical Electrical Incandescent Lamp (1878–79) serves as the starting point for the exhibition, with his historic lightbulb as the primary inspiration. As visitors enter, they are greeted by Ceal Floyer's Light Switch, a slide projection of a switch that invites us to think about the real and the imagined, as well as the blurred boundary between function and illusion. Katie Paterson's Light bulb to Simulate Moonlight connects the power of nature and the vastness of space to a human experience of the everyday.

The perception theme is embodied in Dan Flavin's Untitled (to Don Judd, colourist) 1-5. Flavin is renowned for his groundbreaking use of mass-produced, commercially available fluorescent lights and is considered one of the first artists to embrace the medium of light. Iván Navarro's Manhole creates the optical illusion of three-dimensional space and depth — a captivating work made from lights and mirrors that appears infinite.

Neon is utilised in extraordinary ways as seen in Tracey Emin's It's a crime to live with the person you don't love and Glenn Ligon's black neon Warm Broad Glow. Jenny Holzer's Blue Purple Tilt is a striking work made up of seven LED columns which references the technology used in modern public advertising. Steve McQueen's neon Remember Me adds a poignant reference to the Durham Light Infantry collection, exploring mortality, loss and memory through handwritten versions of the words "remember me."

Science and nature are explored by Olafur Eliasson's large-scale sensory work Stardust particle, named after a microscopic remnant of an exploded star, and Tatsuo Miyajima's Floating Time V2-C–12, which uses the now retro typeface of LED numbers to project a continuous constellation of randomly generated numbers onto a small white table.

🤝 New Commissions and Community

Beyond the established names, visitors are welcomed by a playful, interactive site-specific new commission by Tine Bech, and a new work by Katrin Hanusch which responds to the architecture of the gallery using broken laptop screens — relics of our digital age. Newcastle-based designer Erin McDougle's Two for Joy draws on the old children's nursery rhyme about magpies, traditionally spoken when spotting the bird: "One for sorrow, Two for joy, Three for a girl, Four for a boy, Five for silver, Six for gold, Seven for a secret never to be told."

In the double-height atrium space, Maarten Baas' Real Time, Sweepers' Clock accurately visualises the onward progress of time in a twelve-hour performance of men sweeping rubbish. Visitors thus experience different sensory encounters throughout the exhibition, demonstrating how the simple light bulb can produce a myriad of possibilities.

Tony Guillan, Artistic Director of The Light, said: "It's a rare opportunity to help shape a new kind of cultural space from the ground up — one that brings contemporary artistic practice into dialogue with history, scientific discovery and technological innovation. County Durham and the wider North East have a rich cultural identity, a proud history of innovation and distinctive contemporary voices that deserve a nationally prominent platform." Cllr Karen Allison echoed this sentiment, encouraging residents and visitors alike to enjoy "a wonderful and relaxing place to spend time, whether viewing the amazing artworks, meeting friends for lunch in the café or enjoying a stroll around the grounds."

🔍 长难句解析

① August 2026 marks the opening of The Light in Durham, formerly the Durham Light Infantry (DLI) Museum and Art Gallery, following a significant investment by the venue's owner, Durham County Council, and transformation by award-winning architects NORR.
结构分析：此句为简单句。主语是"August 2026"，谓语是"marks"，宾语是"the opening of The Light in Durham"；其后"formerly the Durham Light Infantry (DLI) Museum and Art Gallery"是同位语（补充说明The Light的前身）；"following a significant investment by the venue's owner, Durham County Council, and transformation by award-winning architects NORR"是介词短语作状语（表示时间/原因，意为"在……之后"），其中"by the venue's owner"和"by award-winning architects NORR"分别修饰investment和transformation表示施动者，"Durham County Council"是"the venue's owner"的同位语。该句通过同位语和介词短语，在一句话中交代了场馆的前身、投资方和改造方。
翻译：2026年8月标志着达勒姆"The Light"的开幕，该场馆前身为达勒姆步兵博物馆与美术馆，此前经场馆所有者达勒姆郡议会重大投资、并由获奖建筑师事务所NORR改造而成。
② The show examines how artists have employed light as a material in their work from the 1960s to the present day, using newly developed technologies ranging from fluorescent tubes and neons to LEDs and digital media.
结构分析：此句为主从复合句。主句是"The show examines how..."，其中how引导宾语从句作examines的宾语。在宾语从句中，主语是"artists"，谓语是"have employed"（现在完成时），宾语是"light"，"as a material"是介词短语作宾语补足语；"in their work"是地点状语，"from the 1960s to the present day"是时间状语；逗号后"using newly developed technologies"是现在分词短语作方式状语，"ranging from fluorescent tubes and neons to LEDs and digital media"是现在分词短语作后置定语（修饰technologies，意为"从荧光灯管和霓虹灯到LED和数字媒体"）。该句通过宾语从句和双重分词结构，完整描述了展览的研究主题与技术手段。
翻译：展览审视了从20世纪60年代至今艺术家如何将光作为创作材料，运用从荧光灯管和霓虹灯到LED和数字媒体等新开发的技术。
③ Iván Navarro's Manhole creates the optical illusion of three-dimensional space and depth — a captivating work made from lights and mirrors that appears infinite.
结构分析：此句为简单句。主语是"Iván Navarro's Manhole"，谓语是"creates"，宾语是"the optical illusion"，其后"of three-dimensional space and depth"是介词短语作后置定语（修饰illusion）；破折号后"a captivating work"是"Manhole"的同位语，"made from lights and mirrors"是过去分词短语作后置定语（修饰work，意为"由灯光和镜子制成的"），"that appears infinite"是that引导的限制性定语从句（修饰work，其中appeals是系动词，infinite是表语，意为"看似无穷无尽的"）。该句通过同位语、过去分词定语和定语从句的层层叠加，对作品进行了生动的描述。
翻译：Iván Navarro的《Manhole》创造了三维空间和深度的视觉错觉——一件由灯光和镜子制成、看似无穷无尽的迷人作品。
④ It's a rare opportunity to help shape a new kind of cultural space from the ground up — one that brings contemporary artistic practice into dialogue with history, scientific discovery and technological innovation, working in collaboration with partners and communities across the county and beyond.
结构分析：此句为主从复合句。主句是"It's a rare opportunity to help shape a new kind of cultural space from the ground up"，其中"It"是形式主语，真正的主语是不定式短语"to help shape a new kind of cultural space from the ground up"（意为"从零开始帮助塑造一种新型文化空间"）；破折号后"one"是"a new kind of cultural space"的同位语，其后"that brings contemporary artistic practice into dialogue with history, scientific discovery and technological innovation"是that引导的限制性定语从句（修饰one，其中"brings...into dialogue with..."是固定搭配，意为"使……与……对话"），其后"working in collaboration with partners and communities across the county and beyond"是现在分词短语作伴随状语。该句通过形式主语、不定式主语、同位语和定语从句的组合，表达了场馆的愿景和使命。
翻译：这是一个从零开始塑造一种新型文化空间的难得机会——它使当代艺术实践与历史、科学发现和技术创新展开对话，并与全县及更广范围内的合作伙伴和社区协作。
