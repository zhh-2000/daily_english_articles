Solar Surpasses Coal: China's Photovoltaic Milestone Marks Energy Turning Point

📅 September 1, 2026  |  📖 约980词  |  ⏱️ 建议阅读时间：15分钟

📋 文章摘要

2026年9月1日，国家能源局宣布中国光伏发电装机容量首次超越煤电，达到12.86亿千瓦，成为装机规模最大的电源品类。太阳能发电量在全社会用电量中占比约13%，即每8度电中有1度来自光伏。这一历史性里程碑标志着中国能源结构从"补充能源"向"主力电源"的跨越。与此同时，印度也实现了300GW非化石燃料装机容量的突破。两国的发展为全球能源转型提供了重要范例，展现了清洁能源在应对气候变化和保障能源安全中的关键作用。

☀️ A Historic Overtake

On September 1, 2026, China's National Energy Administration announced a milestone that may well define the global energy landscape for decades to come: the country's installed solar power capacity has, for the first time in history, surpassed that of coal-fired power. By the end of July, photovoltaic (PV) capacity had reached 1.286 billion kilowatts, edging past coal's 1.285 billion kilowatts to become the single largest power source in the world's most populous nation.

While installed capacity does not equal actual electricity generation, the symbolic weight of this moment cannot be overstated. Coal has long been the backbone of China's industrialisation and urbanisation, providing the reliable, round-the-clock power that fuelled the nation's extraordinary rise. Solar, by contrast, was once dismissed as a marginal "supplementary" source. Its emergence as the leading power source by capacity signals a structural transformation in how the world's second-largest economy produces electricity — one driven by technological progress, strategic planning, and the relentless pressure of rising demand.

📊 The Numbers Behind the Milestone

The data tells a remarkable story of acceleration. Solar now accounts for 31.5% of China's total power capacity. In the first seven months of 2026 alone, solar generation topped 802.4 billion kilowatt-hours, a 15.5% increase over the same period last year. Put differently, roughly one in every eight kilowatt-hours of electricity consumed in China now comes from sunlight. During peak midday hours in summer, solar output can cover as much as one-third of national electricity demand.

This growth has been nothing short of explosive. In the first half of 2026, China added 71.77 million kilowatts of new grid-connected solar capacity, a 16.1% year-on-year increase. The momentum reflects a convergence of factors: surging electricity demand from emerging industries, dramatic cost reductions in solar technology, and a national commitment to peak carbon emissions before 2030 and achieve carbon neutrality before 2060. As computing power demand soars — projected to consume 800 billion kilowatt-hours annually by 2030 — clean energy expansion has become not merely an environmental aspiration but an economic necessity.

🏭 From Factory Floor to Global Dominance

China's solar ascendancy is rooted in an industrial ecosystem that is without parallel. The country dominates every core manufacturing segment of the solar supply chain — from polysilicon and wafers to cells and modules. Today, eight out of every ten photovoltaic modules produced worldwide come from Chinese factories. This manufacturing supremacy has driven down the cost of solar energy to levels that were unimaginable a decade ago, effectively lowering the barrier for clean energy adoption across the developing world.

The benefits extend well beyond domestic borders. In the first half of 2026, China's "new three" export categories — electric vehicles, lithium batteries, and photovoltaic products — grew by 13.3%, 39.9%, and 36.5% respectively. Green development dividends are flowing continuously to global markets, making clean energy more accessible and affordable for nations at every stage of development. Over the next five years, investment in China's solar industry is expected to surpass 2 trillion yuan (approximately 297.6 billion US dollars), promising more green power, stronger energy security, and a cleaner economic engine.

🌱 Cross-Sector Innovation and Rural Benefits

The spread of solar has also spawned innovative cross-sector business models that are transforming rural economies. In the vast Tala Plains of Qinghai Province, a massive photovoltaic park has pioneered a distinctive "solar panels plus Tibetan sheep" model: the panels generate clean electricity above, while the sheltered ground below supports livestock grazing, creating a virtuous cycle of green energy and ecological restoration. Similar "solar-plus-agriculture" and "solar-plus-ecological management" approaches are taking root across the country, linking renewable energy with rural revitalisation in ways that benefit both the environment and local communities.

These integrated models demonstrate that the energy transition is not merely about replacing one power source with another. It is about reimagining the relationship between energy production, land use, agriculture, and community livelihoods — a holistic approach that turns the challenge of decarbonisation into an engine of inclusive growth.

🌐 A Global Pattern: India's Parallel Progress

China's milestone is part of a broader global shift. On August 9, 2026, India's Ministry of New and Renewable Energy announced that the country had crossed 300 gigawatts of non-fossil fuel-based installed electricity generation capacity, reaching 300.50 GW as of July 31. This figure represents over 60% of India's target of 500 GW by 2030, with non-fossil sources now accounting for more than 54% of the nation's total installed capacity of approximately 552 GW.

Solar power is the standout performer in India's clean energy portfolio as well, growing from just 2.8 GW in 2014 to around 165 GW in 2026. During the financial year 2025–26, India added a record 55.29 GW of non-fossil capacity, including 44.6 GW of solar and 6 GW of wind. Both nations, home to over a third of humanity, are demonstrating that rapid industrialisation and clean energy transition can proceed hand in hand — offering a powerful counter-narrative to the assumption that development must come at the expense of the environment.

🔮 Looking Ahead

Despite the celebrations, experts caution that challenges remain. Installed capacity does not translate directly into generation output, as solar's intermittent nature still requires reliable backup from coal, hydro, and nuclear sources. Grid modernisation, energy storage, and demand-side management will be critical to maximising the value of solar power. China's "15th Five-Year Plan" for the new power system envisions that by 2030, non-fossil energy will account for 50% of electricity generation — a target that will require sustained investment and policy coordination.

Nevertheless, the symbolic significance of solar overtaking coal is profound. It marks the moment when renewable energy ceased to be a supplementary footnote and became the main story. For students of energy, economics, and environmental science, this turning point offers a vivid illustration of how technology, policy, and market forces can converge to reshape the foundations of modern civilisation. As one official noted, the achievement is not a simple industry narrative but the product of energy security strategy, structural reform, and independent innovation working in powerful resonance.

🔍 长难句解析

① By the end of July, photovoltaic (PV) capacity had reached 1.286 billion kilowatts, edging past coal's 1.285 billion kilowatts to become the single largest power source in the world's most populous nation.
结构分析：此句为简单句。时间状语"By the end of July"置于句首；主语是"photovoltaic (PV) capacity"，谓语是"had reached"（过去完成时，表示在7月底之前已完成），宾语是"1.286 billion kilowatts"；逗号后"edging past coal's 1.285 billion kilowatts"是现在分词短语作伴随状语（表示伴随主语发生的动作，意为"以微弱优势超过煤电的12.85亿千瓦"），其后"to become the single largest power source"是不定式短语作结果状语（表示"edging past"的结果），"in the world's most populous nation"是介词短语作后置定语（修饰power source，意为"在这个世界人口最多国家的"）。该句通过过去完成时、分词状语和不定式结果状语的组合，在一句话中完整交代了光伏装机的历史性超越。
翻译：截至7月底，光伏装机容量达到12.86亿千瓦，以微弱优势超过煤电的12.85亿千瓦，成为这个世界人口最多国家的最大单一电源。
② This manufacturing supremacy has driven down the cost of solar energy to levels that were unimaginable a decade ago, effectively lowering the barrier for clean energy adoption across the developing world.
结构分析：此句为主从复合句。主句是"This manufacturing supremacy has driven down the cost of solar energy to levels"，其中主语是"This manufacturing supremacy"，谓语是"has driven down"（现在完成时），宾语是"the cost of solar energy"，"to levels"是介词短语作程度状语。其后"that were unimaginable a decade ago"是that引导的限制性定语从句（修饰levels，其中that作主语，were是系动词，unimaginable是表语，"a decade ago"是时间状语）。逗号后"effectively lowering the barrier for clean energy adoption across the developing world"是现在分词短语作结果状语（表示主句动作带来的进一步影响，"effectively"是副词修饰lowering，"for clean energy adoption"是介词短语修饰barrier，"across the developing world"是范围状语）。该句通过定语从句和分词结果状语，生动表达了制造优势带来的双重效应：降低成本与降低门槛。
翻译：这一制造霸主地位将太阳能成本降低到了十年前难以想象的低水平，有效降低了发展中世界采用清洁能源的门槛。
③ In the vast Tala Plains of Qinghai Province, a massive photovoltaic park has pioneered a distinctive "solar panels plus Tibetan sheep" model: the panels generate clean electricity above, while the sheltered ground below supports livestock grazing, creating a virtuous cycle of green energy and ecological restoration.
结构分析：此句为并列复合句。冒号前为主句，时间地点状语"In the vast Tala Plains of Qinghai Province"置于句首，主语是"a massive photovoltaic park"，谓语是"has pioneered"（现在完成时），宾语是"a distinctive 'solar panels plus Tibetan sheep' model"。冒号后为解释性并列句，由while连接两个并列分句构成对比关系：前一分句"the panels generate clean electricity above"（panels为主语，generate为谓语，clean electricity为宾语，above为地点状语）；后一分句"the sheltered ground below supports livestock grazing"（the sheltered ground below为主语，supports为谓语，livestock grazing为宾语）。逗号后"creating a virtuous cycle of green energy and ecological restoration"是现在分词短语作结果状语，修饰整个while并列结构。该句通过冒号解释、while对比和分词结果状语，清晰描述了光伏+牧羊模式的运作原理和生态效益。
翻译：在青海广袤的塔拉滩上，一座大型光伏园区开创了一种独特的"光伏板+藏羊"模式：上方的光伏板产生清洁电力，而下方遮蔽的地面支持牲畜放牧，形成了绿色能源与生态修复的良性循环。
④ Both nations, home to over a third of humanity, are demonstrating that rapid industrialisation and clean energy transition can proceed hand in hand — offering a powerful counter-narrative to the assumption that development must come at the expense of the environment.
结构分析：此句为主从复合句。主句主语是"Both nations"，其后"home to over a third of humanity"是形容词短语作同位语（补充说明两国的人口体量，意为"占人类三分之一以上人口的家园"）；谓语是"are demonstrating"，宾语是that引导的宾语从句"that rapid industrialisation and clean energy transition can proceed hand in hand"（其中rapid industrialisation and clean energy transition为并列主语，can proceed为谓语，hand in hand为方式状语）。破折号后"offering a powerful counter-narrative to the assumption"是现在分词短语作结果状语，其后"that development must come at the expense of the environment"是that引导的同位语从句（解释assumption的内容，意为"发展必以牺牲环境为代价的假设"）。该句通过同位语、宾语从句、分词状语和同位语从句的多层嵌套，表达了两国实践对传统发展观的挑战。
翻译：两国都是占人类三分之一以上人口的家园，它们正在证明，快速工业化与清洁能源转型可以齐头并进——为"发展必以牺牲环境为代价"的假设提供了有力的反面论调。
