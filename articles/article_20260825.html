Caltech Breakthrough Brings Fiber-Optic Performance to Silicon Chips

📅 August 25, 2026  |  📖 约950词  |  ⏱️ 建议阅读时间：15分钟

📋 文章摘要

2026年8月，加州理工学院研究团队在《自然》杂志发表论文，宣布成功将光纤级超低损耗性能移植到硅芯片上。研究团队使用与光纤相同的锗硅酸盐玻璃，通过螺旋形波导设计和"回流"工艺，在可见光波段将信号损耗较主流氮化硅平台降低20倍，激光相干性提升超100倍。该突破为光子集成电路在AI数据中心、量子计算、光钟和原子传感器等领域的应用铺平道路，标志着芯片技术向更高能效、更高精度迈进。

💡 From Optical Fiber to Silicon Chips

Optical fiber already forms much of the hidden infrastructure behind modern communications. It allows information to travel over long distances at high speed because the glass inside the fiber is extremely pure and its surface is engineered to be exceptionally smooth. As a result, most of the light entering one end can reach the other without being absorbed, scattered, or otherwise lost. Researchers refer to this as ultralow loss performance.

For years, scientists have dreamed of bringing this fiber-like efficiency directly onto the silicon wafers used for computer chips. Now, a team led by Professor Kerry Vahala at the California Institute of Technology (Caltech) has done exactly that. "We have developed a method to print optical circuits, made from the same material as optical fiber, directly onto the same 8- and 12-inch wafers used for computer chips," Vahala explains. "This shift toward fiber-like performance, especially in the visible bands, will enable new technologies that benefit from negligibly low circuit energy loss."

🔧 How the Breakthrough Works

The key lies in the material and the manufacturing technique. The researchers create waveguides — nanoscale on-chip pathways that channel light — using germano-silicate, the same type of glass used in optical fiber. They adapt this material to a lithography-based method suitable for making devices on standard semiconductor wafers, meaning the process is compatible with existing chip factories.

Instead of running in a straight line, the waveguides are arranged in spirals. This allows light to travel a much longer optical path while remaining inside a very small area. The idea resembles winding optical fiber around a spool, but nanofabrication makes it possible to fit the path into a footprint only a few millimeters across.

A crucial step comes after the waveguides are patterned. Because germano-silicate has a comparatively low melting temperature, the team can place their devices into a furnace to "reflow" the surface. This process smooths the waveguide walls down to the level of individual atoms, largely suppressing the severe scattering loss that has limited conventional visible-light photonic chips. "At visible wavelengths, our recent platform exceeds silicon nitride's record by a factor of 20, and we have more room to improve," says Hao-Jing Chen, a lead author of the study.

📊 A 20-Fold Advantage at Visible Wavelengths

The performance gap between the new platform and existing technology is striking. At near-infrared wavelengths, devices built with the new Caltech platform already match the performance of some of the best previous devices made from silicon nitride, a material widely used in optical technology because it can transmit data with relatively little signal loss.

The advantage becomes much larger at visible wavelengths, where the new germano-silicate platform substantially surpasses silicon nitride. Reducing loss has a dramatic effect on the performance of optical devices. Lasers produced with the new platform, for example, show more than a 100-fold improvement over previous designs in how long their light remains coherent — meaning the light waves stay in step with one another for far longer, a property essential for precision measurement and communication.

Every factor of 10 decrease in loss produces a factor of 100 improvement in coherence. This exponential relationship explains why the research community treats even modest reductions in waveguide loss as major milestones.

🌐 From Optical Clocks to Quantum Technology

The ability to create ultralow-loss waveguides across visible wavelengths could be useful for many different technologies. "One of the reasons this is so compelling is that it has a Swiss Army-knife quality — it can be applied in a wide range of settings," Vahala says. The expanded wavelength coverage supports many important atomic operations, making chip-scale atomic sensors, optical clocks, and ion-trap quantum systems possible.

The germano-silicate waveguides can also be efficiently coupled to both optical fibers and semiconductor lasers, a property that Henry Blauvelt, a visiting associate at Caltech and co-author of the paper, describes as being "of paramount importance in reducing the overall energy cost of server infrastructure." As data centers consume ever-growing amounts of electricity to power artificial intelligence workloads, this efficiency gain could translate into enormous energy savings across the global digital economy.

To demonstrate the platform's versatility, the researchers describe several devices made with the new material in their Nature paper, including ring resonators, multiple types of lasers, and nonlinear resonators capable of generating a range of frequencies. The team sees the current results as an early stage rather than an endpoint. "We haven't gone as far as we want to go, but we've made significant progress over the last five years, and that's what we're reporting on here," Vahala notes.

🚀 Why Tiny Chips Need Kilometer-Scale Performance

At first, the effort to measure losses over distances of meters or even kilometers might seem excessive for devices that fit on a small chip. Graduate student Kellan Colburn, a lead author of the study, admits that it might seem "a little ridiculous" that the researchers are aiming for losses that can be described by percentages over kilometers. "After all, our chips are only 2 centimeters across. But, in reality, there are a lot of applications where this would be very powerful," he says.

One example is the ring resonator, a basic optical device used in both scientific research and data transmission. Light enters the device and is directed into a ring, where it can continue circulating for an extended period. Although the physical ring may measure only a few millimeters, the total effective distance traveled by the light depends on how little energy is lost each time it goes around. The longer light can circulate, the higher the performance of the resulting devices — whether they are lasers, sensors, or communication components.

The work, funded by the Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency, the Air Force Research Laboratory, and international partners, signals that the boundary between fiber optics and chip-scale photonics is steadily dissolving. As manufacturing techniques mature, the same low-loss performance that once spanned oceans may soon be printed onto the silicon inside every computer, bringing light — and its extraordinary efficiency — to the heart of modern technology.

🔍 长难句解析

① Most of the light entering one end can reach the other without being absorbed, scattered, or otherwise lost.
结构分析：此句为简单句，但修饰成分丰富。主语是"Most of the light"，其后跟现在分词短语"entering one end"作后置定语（修饰light，意为"进入一端的光"）；谓语是"can reach"，宾语是"the other"（指另一端）；"without being absorbed, scattered, or otherwise lost"是介词短语作方式状语，其中being absorbed、scattered和otherwise lost是三个并列的被动语态动名词，由or连接，说明光在传播过程中没有被吸收、散射或损耗。该句通过分词定语和并列被动结构，简洁而精确地描述了光纤低损耗传输的特性。
翻译：进入一端的大部分光能够到达另一端，而不会被吸收、散射或以其他方式损耗。
② We have developed a method to print optical circuits, made from the same material as optical fiber, directly onto the same 8- and 12-inch wafers used for computer chips.
结构分析：此句为简单句，但含多个修饰成分。主句是"We have developed a method"；不定式短语"to print optical circuits ... directly onto the same 8- and 12-inch wafers used for computer chips"作method的后置定语（或目的状语），其中"made from the same material as optical fiber"是过去分词短语作circuits的后置定语（说明光路的材料与光纤相同），"used for computer chips"是过去分词短语作wafers的后置定语（说明晶圆用途）。"directly onto"中onto为介词，表示方向。该句通过层层嵌套的分词定语，清晰说明了新方法的材料、工艺与载体三个关键要素。
翻译：我们开发了一种方法，将采用与光纤相同材料制成的光路直接印制到用于计算机芯片的8英寸和12英寸晶圆上。
③ Due to the comparatively low melting temperature of the material, we can put our devices into a furnace to "reflow" the surface of our waveguides to get their smoothness down to the level of individual atoms, which largely suppresses the severe scattering loss that has limited conventional visible PICs.
结构分析：此句为主从复合句，结构较复杂。"Due to the comparatively low melting temperature of the material"是介词短语作原因状语；主句是"we can put our devices into a furnace"，其后"to 'reflow' the surface of our waveguides"是不定式短语作目的状语，"to get their smoothness down to the level of individual atoms"是第二个不定式短语作目的状语（表示进一步的目的）；"which largely suppresses the severe scattering loss"是which引导的非限制性定语从句，修饰前面整个reflow过程；"that has limited conventional visible PICs"是that引导的限制性定语从句，修饰scattering loss。该句通过"due to + 目的状语 + 非限制性定语从句 + 限制性定语从句"的多层结构，完整呈现了原因、方法、目的和效果之间的逻辑链条。
翻译：由于该材料的熔点相对较低，我们可以将器件放入炉中，对波导表面进行"回流"处理，使其光滑度达到单个原子的水平，这大幅抑制了此前限制传统可见光光子集成电路的严重散射损耗。
④ As manufacturing techniques mature, the same low-loss performance that once spanned oceans may soon be printed onto the silicon inside every computer, bringing light — and its extraordinary efficiency — to the heart of modern technology.
结构分析：此句为主从复合句。"As manufacturing techniques mature"是as引导的时间状语从句（意为"随着制造技术成熟"）；主句是"the same low-loss performance ... may soon be printed onto the silicon inside every computer"，其中"that once spanned oceans"是that引导的定语从句，修饰performance（意为"曾经跨越海洋的"），主句谓语"may soon be printed"是被动语态；"bringing light — and its extraordinary efficiency — to the heart of modern technology"是现在分词短语作结果状语，其中破折号内的"and its extraordinary efficiency"是插入语，与light并列，作bringing的宾语。该句以as开头营造时间递进感，通过定语从句和分词结果状语，将技术突破的意义从"曾经"延伸到"即将"，富有文学色彩。
翻译：随着制造技术成熟，曾经跨越海洋的低损耗性能可能很快被印制到每台计算机内部的硅芯片上，将光——及其非凡的效率——带到现代技术的核心。
